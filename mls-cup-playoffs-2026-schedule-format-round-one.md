## MLS Playoffs Format: How the 18-Team Bracket Works

![Bracket funnel from the Wild Card to MLS Cup showing how many teams remain at each stage](/images/playoffs26-section-5.jpg)

Nine teams from each conference qualify. The top seven in the East and the top seven in the West go straight into Round One. Seeds 8 and 9 in each conference play a single-game Wild Card match. The loser is out. The winner plays the conference’s No. 1 seed in Round One: Nashville SC in the East and Vancouver Whitecaps in the West.

After Round One, the format changes to single-elimination matches, with the higher seed hosting every match:

1. **Round One:** best-of-three series
2. **Conference Semifinals:** one match
3. **Conference Finals:** one match, the highest remaining seed against the lowest
4. **MLS Cup:** one match, hosted by the finalist with the better regular-season record

## How Round One Works: The Best-of-3 Explained

![Diagram of the Round One best-of-three: who hosts Game 1, Game 2 and Game 3, with Nashville SC vs FC Cincinnati as an example](/images/playoffs26-section-4.jpg)

Round One is a best-of-three series, like a short cricket series. The first team to win two matches moves on, and the loser’s season ends.

- **Game 1:** the higher seed hosts.
- **Game 2:** the lower seed hosts, so both teams get at least one home match.
- **Game 3:** played only if the series is 1-1. The higher seed hosts again.
- **No draws:** every match has a winner, and there is no aggregate score.
- **No extra time:** if a match is level after 90 minutes, it goes straight to a penalty shootout.

**Example: Nashville SC vs FC Cincinnati.** Nashville host Game 1 on Sunday 22 November. Cincinnati host Game 2 on Saturday 28 November. If each team wins once, Nashville host the deciding Game 3 on Wednesday 2 December. If one team wins both games, the series is over after Game 2.
