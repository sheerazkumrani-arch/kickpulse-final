## MLS matchups: what is at stake in the Wild Card

Both clubs finished the regular season on 46 points, which is why the seeding came down to the final day. Orlando ended up eighth with 13 wins, 14 defeats and 7 draws, while Cincinnati took the last Eastern Conference spot in ninth with 12 wins, 12 defeats and 10 draws.

The prize is big. The winner moves into a best-of-three series against Supporters' Shield champions Nashville SC, which starts on 22 November. There is no extra time in this game. If it is level after 90 minutes, it goes straight to a penalty shootout.

## MLS scores today: form and head to head

Orlando go into the game having beaten New England 1-0 in their last outing, and they have been strong at home lately. Cincinnati lost 0-2 to Nashville on the same day.

The 2026 meetings between these two have been wildly different. Cincinnati won 6-2 in May, and the August game in Orlando finished 1-1. Expect a tighter game this time, but both defences have leaked goals. Cincinnati scored 67 goals this season and conceded 75, so goals at both ends are a real possibility.

## Team news and predicted lineups

Official lineups drop about an hour before kickoff, and the league's Player Availability Report is the place to check late injury and suspension news. Until then, here is what we know.

- **Orlando City:** Antoine Griezmann arrived from Atlético Madrid in July and has been the story of their second-half turnaround. Martín Ojeda is the club's all-time leading scorer, and 19-year-old Justin Ellis has broken through as a forward. Goalkeeper Maxime Crépeau finished the season with 137 saves.
- **FC Cincinnati:** Evander (14 goals, 17 assists) and Kévin Denkey (17 goals) lead an attack that scores in bunches. Their back line has been rotated all year, so watch who starts in defence.
- **Likely shapes:** Orlando are expected to line up in a 4-2-3-1. Cincinnati used a 3-4-3 in August, with Denkey and Evander up front, and could stay with it. Treat both as guides, not confirmed lineups.

## Key matchup: Griezmann vs Cincinnati's back three

The biggest question is whether Cincinnati's three centre-backs can handle Griezmann dropping into the half-spaces between their wing-backs and centre-backs. If Orlando's double pivot wins the ball early, quick passes to Ellis and Ojeda can punish a team that pushes its wide players high. If Cincinnati control the middle, Evander's passing to Denkey becomes the danger.
