Pitchside Notes is written by one person, [Sheeraz Khan](/author/sheeraz-khan/). This page explains honestly how articles are made. I started writing recently and I am still improving.

## Research

Before writing, I research the teams myself. I look at:

- each team's recent form,
- how they play (attacking or defensive football) and their strategy,
- the head-to-head history,
- how they performed in big matches before, for example how a team handled similar pressure.

For facts like scores, lineups, dates, kick-off times and tables, I use ESPN, BBC Sport and other reliable sports websites. I also watch many of the big matches myself. If I use a quote, it comes from a named source.

## How I use AI (Claude)

I use Claude only as an assistant. It helps me with ideas, with improving my draft, and with making Urdu versions of my articles and suggestions in Urdu. I explain what I want to write, Claude gives ideas or suggestions, and then I write the points in my own way.

AI is not the final editor. The analysis and the final decisions are mine.

## Checking before publishing

After writing, I check the article again and again myself before I publish: whether the facts are correct, how it is written, and whether the analysis makes sense.

## Pictures

I make the analysis graphics myself with Canva. They show things like how many goals a team has conceded, how strong the attack and defence of both teams are, and how many goals might be scored.

## Predictions

Predictions are my own opinion based on the research above. They are not betting advice.

## Corrections

If you find a mistake, please tell me by email ([s.khanu1988@gmail.com](mailto:s.khanu1988@gmail.com)) or [message me on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/923125353439). I will correct real mistakes.

When I correct an article, I add a visible note at the top: **"Updated on [date] to correct: [what was wrong]"**, so readers can see what changed.

## Updates

When I add new information to an article (for example the result after a preview), the article shows an "Updated" date under the headline.

## Independence

Pitchside Notes is not connected to any league, club, federation or broadcaster. There are no ads or paid articles on the site at the moment.
