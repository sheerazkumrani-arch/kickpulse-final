This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you visit Pitchside Notes (kickpulse-final.vercel.app). The site is run by Sheeraz Khan. Contact: [s.khanu1988@gmail.com](mailto:s.khanu1988@gmail.com).

## Short version

- You can read every article without an account. The site does not ask for your name or email.
- There are **no ads** and **no affiliate links** on this site at the moment.
- The site does **not set its own cookies**.

## Visit statistics (Vercel Analytics)

The site is hosted by Vercel. It uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits (for example, which pages are read and from which country). According to Vercel, this works without cookies and does not identify you personally. It helps me see which articles are useful.

## Hosting

Like every website host, Vercel's servers receive technical information when you open a page, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the page and keep it secure.

## Fonts from Google

The text styles (fonts) are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, so Google receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for details.

## Live scores box

The home page has a box with today's MLS matches. Your browser loads these scores directly from ESPN's public feed, so ESPN receives your IP address.

## Links to other websites

Articles link to other websites (for example official clubs, leagues and news sites) and include share buttons for X, Facebook, WhatsApp and LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy rules. The share buttons are simple links: nothing is sent to them unless you click.

## Email

If you email me, I use your message and email address only to reply to you. I do not sell or share them.

## Changes

If I add advertising (for example Google AdSense) or anything that uses cookies, I will update this page before it starts. The date at the top shows the last update.
