Pitchside Notes is a football website written by [Sheeraz Khan](/author/sheeraz-khan/). It publishes match previews, match reports and analysis about the Premier League, MLS, La Liga, international football and other big matches.

## Who writes this site

My name is Sheeraz Khan. I am from Dhoro Naro, Sindh, Pakistan. Besides writing about football, I work as a school teacher and teach classes 1 to 5. I have a Master's in Economics (MA), a Diploma in IT and a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed). I am passionate about computers, and I am learning to build useful websites and tools. This site is one of them.

## How my love for football started

I started following football in 2015, when I began playing the game Dream League Soccer. I bought Messi and Ronaldo with in-game coins and enjoyed playing with them. Then I started following the real players and clubs. Messi's goals in that game made Barcelona my favourite club.

Today I follow almost every big club and national team, including Barcelona, Real Madrid, Manchester United and Spain.

## What I follow most

- **Barcelona** is my favourite club. I have supported them for many years.
- **Premier League:** I follow Manchester City, and I enjoy Premier League matches more than any other league.
- **MLS** is my favourite league to watch, because of Lionel Messi, my favourite player, and because big players like Griezmann and Suarez have moved there since his transfer.
- I also follow **La Liga** and the big European matches.

## Why this site exists

I wanted to write football articles that go beyond simple news. I research first, pick one clear point, and explain it with pictures and analysis. I write before and after matches, so readers get articles on time.

Most big matches (MLS, La Liga and other major games) are played at night in Pakistan time. I often stay awake until about 4 AM to watch them, so I can write and publish on time.

I started writing recently and I keep working to improve my articles. You can read how I research and check articles in the [Editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Independent site

Pitchside Notes is an independent fan site. It is not connected to any league, club, federation or broadcaster. Predictions are my opinions, not betting advice.
