I am Sheeraz Khan, the writer of Pitchside Notes. I am from Dhoro Naro, Sindh, Pakistan, and I work as a school teacher (classes 1 to 5). I have a Master's in Economics (MA), a Diploma in IT and a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed).

I started following football in 2015 through the game Dream League Soccer, and Messi's goals in that game made Barcelona my favourite club. In the Premier League I follow Manchester City. MLS is my favourite league to watch because of Lionel Messi, my favourite player. I also follow La Liga and the big European matches.

I write in an analytical style: I research the teams, point out what matters, and explain it with my own graphics. Many matches are played at night in Pakistan time, so I often stay awake until about 4 AM to watch them and publish on time.
