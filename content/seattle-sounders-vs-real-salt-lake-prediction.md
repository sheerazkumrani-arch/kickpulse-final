## 2. Recent Form: Seattle Sounders vs Real Salt Lake

Form does not decide a playoff game on its own, but it is the best place to start. Here is how the two sides arrived.

![Seattle Sounders vs Real Salt Lake recent form comparison](/images/seattle-rsl-comparison.jpg)

**Seattle Sounders** ended the regular season with a 2-1 win over LAFC. That result locked in a playoff spot and home-field advantage for the Wild Card round.

Their last five league results:

- Seattle Sounders 2-1 LAFC
- Vancouver Whitecaps 5-1 Seattle Sounders
- Seattle Sounders 0-1 Houston Dynamo
- San Diego FC 2-2 Seattle Sounders
- Seattle Sounders 1-1 CF Montréal

Seattle have conceded ten goals across those five games. The LAFC win is encouraging, but the defensive record is a concern.

**Real Salt Lake** come in with a stronger run. Their last five league results:

- Real Salt Lake 2-1 LA Galaxy
- San Jose Earthquakes 2-1 Real Salt Lake
- Austin FC 1-2 Real Salt Lake
- Real Salt Lake 3-0 St. Louis CITY SC
- Sporting Kansas City 1-3 Real Salt Lake

That is four wins from five, with 11 goals scored and five conceded.

So the picture is clear. Seattle are at home, but Real Salt Lake have been the more consistent team lately.

The last meeting is worth remembering too. In September, Seattle beat Real Salt Lake 2-0 at home, which shows they can cause problems for this opponent at Lumen Field.

## 3. Team News and Injury Updates

Availability could decide this match, and Seattle have the bigger worry.

**Seattle Sounders:** Defender Jackson Ragen has been given a two-match suspension by the MLS Disciplinary Committee after an incident in the LAFC game on November 7. He will miss the Wild Card match. Ragen has been a key part of the back line this season, so this is a real blow.

Yeimar Gómez Andrade has been dealing with a hamstring issue. There are signs he could return, but his fitness should be checked before kickoff.

There is still quality elsewhere. Cristian Roldan brings experience and energy in midfield, and Dejan Joveljić remains a dangerous attacking option.

**Real Salt Lake:** Midfielder Diego Luna had meniscus repair surgery in September and was expected to miss the rest of the 2026 season. That takes away some creativity.

Even so, the goals have kept coming. Sergi Solans has 15 goals and four assists, and Morgan Guilavogui gives the attack movement and support.

Injury news can change quickly, so check the final team news close to kickoff.

## 5. Key Matchup: Seattle's Defence vs Real Salt Lake's Attack

The game may come down to one battle: a Seattle defence without Ragen against a Real Salt Lake attack that has been scoring freely.

Real Salt Lake will look for quick passes and runs in behind the defensive line. Solans is the player to watch, because 15 goals this season shows he knows where the chances are.

Seattle will need Roldan and Albert Rusnák to control the middle of the pitch and stop RSL from getting too many shots on goal. Rusnák's passing could be important when Seattle go forward, and Joveljić will need good service to turn possession into goals.

Seattle's September 2-0 win gives them some confidence. But with RSL in such good attacking form, any careless moment at the back could be punished.
