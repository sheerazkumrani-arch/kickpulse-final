# Inter Miami vs Columbus Crew: Why Messi's Champions Are Out

Inter Miami are out of the 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs. The defending champions lost 1-0 to Columbus Crew in Game 2 of their Round One series on Friday night, and Santiago Rodríguez's goal two minutes into stoppage time settled the tie 2-0. For long spells Miami had more of the ball and the better looks at goal. This Inter Miami vs Columbus Crew analysis explains why that was still not enough.

## Inter Miami vs Columbus Crew: The Night Miami's Title Defence Ended

It was 38°F at kick-off in Columbus, with rain throughout. Miami arrived already 1-0 down in the best-of-three series after a 3-0 home defeat at Nu Stadium on 21 November.

Win, and the series went back to Miami for Game 3. Lose, and the champions were out. A draw meant penalties.

Columbus, the No. 7 seed, were no ordinary underdog. Laurent Courtois' side were bottom of the East on 1 September, yet arrived on a seven-match winning run.

## How the Match Unfolded: From Hope to a Stoppage-Time Winner

Miami started the brighter side. Columbus defended passively in the first half, and Messi's free kick from around 30 yards in the 22nd minute forced Patrick Schulte to tip the ball over. A minute earlier Brais Méndez had curled a shot that Dayne St. Clair blocked.

Schulte also saved from Fricio Caicedo in the 40th minute.

The second half was a series of near misses. In the 64th minute St. Clair saved Gonzalo Tapia's header, and Micael cleared Méndez's rebound off the line. In the 75th minute Casemiro turned in a Messi free kick, but he was offside and the goal was ruled out. In the 84th minute Germán Berterame, unmarked, headed over.

Then, in the 92nd minute, Anass Zaroury crossed and Rodríguez finished from close range after a scramble.

## Lionel Messi's Performance Against Columbus Crew: Did He Get Enough Support?

Messi played the full match and was Miami's main source of ideas. Athlon Sports noted that he created most of the few chances Miami had, but he did not land a shot on target. His best moments were two set pieces: the free kick that Schulte saved, and the delivery that Casemiro put in the net from an offside position.

ESPN reported that Columbus limited his space across both games, and manager Kily González admitted Messi had not threatened the goal much.

The real issue was what happened around him. Berterame, Miami's big pre-season signing, missed two chances, including the late header. When your best player is crowded out, the chances he does create must be taken. Messi finished 2026 with 26 goals and 17 assists; over two playoff games, Miami did not score once.

## Tactical Analysis: Why Inter Miami Lost Against Columbus Crew

The match data shows a tight game. Miami had 52% of possession to Columbus' 48%. Columbus had eight shots to Miami's six, and each side had three on target.

González described the problem clearly. He said Columbus understood how Miami played and sat behind a deep, five-man defensive line that Miami could not break.

Our view: the key was where Columbus defended. Columbus gave up the middle third and protected the penalty area. That turned Miami's possession into crosses and set pieces. Two of Miami's three best chances came from dead balls, which suggests open play rarely reached the box.

Columbus, meanwhile, needed only a few clean attacks. Rodríguez completed seven dribbles and took four shots, according to Athlon. When the game opened up late, he was the one in the right place. Miami's late push left space behind, and Columbus used it.

## Inter Miami's Elimination Explained: A Season That Ran Out of Answers

The defeat confirmed a pattern. ESPN noted that Miami had a poor home record this season and only one win against another playoff team since mid-July.

This is the first time Miami have gone out of the first round in just two games, and the first time they have failed to score in consecutive games since 2023. In 2024 they lost in the first round too, but only after a Game 3 against Atlanta United.

Did they fight? Their manager thought so. González said the team "rose to the occasion" and at times outplayed Columbus, but lacked finesse in the final third. He also said: "We created the clearest scoring opportunities. But that's football for you."

Courtois credited his squad for sticking to the plan. Columbus have now won eight in a row and outscored the champions 4-0.

## What Next for Messi and Inter Miami?

Columbus move on to the Eastern Conference Semifinals against New England Revolution or Philadelphia Union. Miami begin a long off-season.

ESPN reports that the club is expected to announce end-of-season roster decisions in the coming days. Miami will start the 2027 season at home to FC Cincinnati on 19 February. They also have the MLS sprint season, the Concacaf Champions Cup and the Leagues Cup to plan for.

The lesson from this series is clear. Miami need a way to break down a deep, organised defence when the space around Messi has gone.
