# Inter Miami Playoffs: Can Messi Save the Title Defence?

![Inter Miami playoffs hero banner: MIA 0-3 CLB in Game 1, Messi 26 league goals, 60 points, 59 goals conceded, Game 2 must-win on 27 Nov](/images/inter-miami-playoffs-crisis-hero-banner.jpg)

**Executive summary:** Inter Miami's playoff run is in crisis. The defending MLS Cup champions lost 3-0 at home to the seventh-seeded Columbus Crew in Game 1 of their best-of-three series. They must now win in Columbus on Friday, 27 November, to stay alive. Lionel Messi has just won a second straight Golden Boot. Even so, a coaching change, a leaky defence and an early red card have left Miami on the brink. For fans following MLS results and MLS matches today, this is the series to watch.

**Key events timeline**
- **Dec 2025:** Miami beat Vancouver 3-1 for their first MLS Cup. Messi provided two assists.
- **14 Apr 2026:** Javier Mascherano resigns, citing "personal reasons".
- **14 Apr–14 Sep:** Guillermo Hoyos is interim coach. Kily González takes over on 15 September.
- **16 Sep:** Miami beat Cruz Azul 2-0 to win the Campeones Cup. Messi scores his 100th club goal.
- **7 Nov:** The regular season ends with a 3-3 draw against Charlotte.
- **21 Nov:** Miami lose Game 1 to Columbus 0-3.

## Inter Miami's Current Situation

![Inter Miami current situation: Eastern Conference top five with Miami 2nd on 60 points, Game 1 timeline of the 0-3 loss to Columbus Crew, Game 2 on 27 November](/images/inter-miami-playoffs-crisis-section-1-current-situation.jpg)

Miami finished the regular season 16W-6L-12D on 60 points. They scored 80 goals and conceded 59. That put them 2nd in the East and 3rd overall, 17 points behind Supporters' Shield winners Nashville.

Game 1 at Nu Stadium went wrong early. Gonzalo Tapia scored a 9th-minute penalty after goalkeeper Dayne St. Clair brought him down. Gonzalo Luján was then sent off for a second yellow card in the 36th minute. Taha Habroune (52') and Tapia (58') completed the win, and Patrick Schulte made five saves, several of them against Messi.

Messi was booked for throwing the ball at Tapia. Columbus's coach said it deserved a red card. Game 2 is Friday at 8 pm ET in Columbus. A Game 3, if needed, would be in Miami on 2 December. Luján is expected to miss Game 2 under standard MLS red-card rules (not officially confirmed).

## How Inter Miami Got Here

![Root-cause timeline for the Inter Miami playoffs crisis: MLS Cup win, Messi strain, Champions Cup exit, Mascherano resignation, Leagues Cup exit, Kily González appointed; 3 coaches, 12 draws, 59 goals conceded](/images/inter-miami-playoffs-crisis-section-2-root-causes.jpg)

**Facts:**
- Mascherano left four months after winning the title, and Miami used three head coaches in one season.
- Miami drew 12 games, more than any other top-five team in the East. They also conceded 59 goals, compared with Nashville's 25.
- Messi, now 39, picked up a muscle strain in preseason.
- Miami went out of the Concacaf Champions Cup to Nashville on away goals and were eliminated in the Leagues Cup league phase.
- The squad relies heavily on veterans Luis Suárez, Rodrigo De Paul and 34-year-old Casemiro.

**Opinion:** ESPN argued that Mascherano's exit exposed the club's delicate, Messi-centric balancing act. Forbes suggested the resignation "feels forced". Both are media views, not established facts.

## Similar Situations in the Past

![Comparison cards: Inter Miami 2023 before Messi then Leagues Cup win, 2024 Supporters' Shield winners knocked out by Atlanta United, Columbus Crew 2026 recovery from last in the East](/images/inter-miami-playoffs-crisis-section-3-past-slumps.jpg)

Miami have been here before. In 2024 they won the Supporters' Shield and were then knocked out in Round One by Atlanta United. MLSsoccer.com openly asked whether 2024 was repeating itself. In 2023, before Messi arrived, the club's first major trophy was still to come. They won the Leagues Cup that summer after his debut.

Columbus are a comeback story of their own. They were last in the East on 1 September, then won eight of nine matches (including six in a row) to reach the playoffs. In short series, current form counts for more than reputation.

## Messi's Achievements at Inter Miami

![Lionel Messi achievements at Inter Miami: four trophies 2023-2026, two MLS MVP awards, two Golden Boots, 29 goals in 2025, 26 goals in 2026, 43 goal contributions, 100 club goals](/images/inter-miami-playoffs-crisis-section-4-messi-achievements.jpg)

- **Trophies:** Leagues Cup 2023, Supporters' Shield 2024, MLS Cup 2025, Campeones Cup 2026
- **Awards:** MLS MVP in 2024 and 2025, the first player to win it in consecutive seasons. Golden Boot in 2025 (29 goals, 19 assists) and in 2026 (26 goals, 17 assists, a league-best 43 goal contributions), again the first back-to-back winner
- **Milestones:** Fastest player in MLS history to reach 100 regular-season goal contributions. Scored his 100th Miami goal in the Campeones Cup final
- **Matches he decided in 2026:** two goals in a 3-2 win at Colorado, four goals in the 7-1 rout of Montréal, two goals in a 3-1 win at Atlanta, and a late goal in the 2-1 comeback win at New England

## The Way Out: How Inter Miami Can Recover

![Inter Miami recovery scenarios panel: best case, base case and worst case for the Round One series vs Columbus, plus tactical, Messi, defensive and 2027 levers](/images/inter-miami-playoffs-crisis-section-5-recovery-scenarios.jpg)

**Tactics:** Miami need a deeper, more compact midfield block, and they must cut out the rash challenges that decided Game 1. With Luján likely out, González needs a settled centre-back pairing.

**Messi's role:** He is still Miami's best creator. At 39, though, he cannot do 90 minutes of pressing. He works best playing centrally near goal, with younger runners such as Telasco Segovia and Germán Berterame around him.

**Next season:** Rely less on veterans, put more Designated Player resources into defence, and give González a full pre-season.

**Scenarios:**
- **Best case:** Miami win in Columbus, win Game 3 at home and go on a run.
- **Base case:** They force a Game 3, but the defensive problems remain.
- **Worst case:** A defeat on Friday ends the title defence in Round One.
