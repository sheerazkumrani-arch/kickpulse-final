## Pochettino's record against Liverpool: 3 wins in 19, and what he does best

![Pochettino record against Liverpool card: 19 matches, 3 wins, 5 draws, 11 defeats, winless in last 8, one Premier League win at Anfield in 2013](/images/pochettino-record-against-liverpool-anfield-debut-section-1-pochettino-record.jpg)

Across Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea, Pochettino has faced Liverpool 19 times. He has won 3, drawn 5 and lost 11. His teams scored 20 goals and conceded 33.

He has not beaten Liverpool in his last eight attempts (two draws, six defeats). In the Premier League at Anfield, he has won once in eight visits, a 1-0 win with Southampton in September 2013. He has lost more Premier League games to Liverpool (eight) than to any other club. The most painful defeat came in the 2019 Champions League final with Tottenham.

Pochettino is also the first United manager whose first league game is against Liverpool.

**What he does best (facts from his record):**
- **Pressing and intensity.** His Southampton and Spurs teams pressed high and played quick, vertical passes. Spurs regularly ranked among the league's top teams for distance covered.
- **Developing young players.** Harry Kane and Dele Alli flourished under him. At Chelsea, Cole Palmer scored 22 Premier League goals and made 11 assists in 2023/24.
- **A settled shape.** He has mostly used a 4-2-3-1 at Southampton, Spurs and Chelsea. With the USA at the 2026 World Cup, his team averaged eight high turnovers per game and reached the last 16.

**Opinion:** His record against Liverpool shows the limits of pressing against an elite side. At United he inherits a squad that ranks 19th for distance covered, so a full high press is unlikely in week one.
