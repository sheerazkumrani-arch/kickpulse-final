## What is at stake in Group A2

The first meeting in Amsterdam on 24 September finished 1-1. Felix Nmecha put Germany ahead, and Cody Gakpo equalised in stoppage time. It was the first game in charge for both Klopp and Xavi.

Heading into the final round, the Netherlands lead the group after a 7-0 win over Greece, while Germany arrive on the back of a 3-1 win in Serbia. The top two sides in each League A group reach the quarter-finals in March 2027, so a draw is enough for the Dutch but Germany almost certainly need all three points.

## Tactical outlook: Klopp’s pressing vs Dutch creativity

A Klopp-coached side brings an unmistakable identity: aggressive counter-pressing, fast vertical transitions, and relentless pressure on the opposition’s full-backs. For Germany, the key to controlling this **Germany vs Netherlands Nations League** tie is winning second balls in central areas and releasing pace into the channels the moment possession turns over.

The Netherlands, now under Xavi Hernández, thrive when they break that first wave of pressure. Their attackers look to pull centre-backs out of position and slide quick combinations into wide runners. If Germany’s midfield pivot leaves space behind the press, **Netherlands vs Germany** matches quickly turn into open, end-to-end games.
