> **Quick answer:** Liverpool beat Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday 22 November 2026. Leny Yoro was sent off after eight minutes, and Liverpool were 2-0 up by the 13th minute through Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz. Rio Ngumoha and substitute Victor Muñoz added the rest in Mauricio Pochettino's first match as United manager.

*Last updated: 22 November 2026, 23:55 PKT. Final score confirmed.*

**Key takeaways**
- **Result:** Liverpool 4-0 Manchester United (half-time 2-0), Premier League matchweek 11
- **Turning point:** Yoro's straight red card for a last-man foul on Alexander Isak (8')
- **Goals:** Szoboszlai 10' (free-kick), Wirtz 13', Ngumoha 66', Muñoz 82'
- **Numbers:** Liverpool had 28 shots to 10 and 4.38 xG to 0.63, says Sky Sports
- **Record:** Opta says 2-0 after 12 minutes 40 seconds was Liverpool's fastest two-goal lead over United in the Premier League
- **Table:** Liverpool move to 21 points. United stay on 8, in the bottom three

## Result summary: Liverpool 4-0 Man United

![Liverpool 4-0 Man United scoreline card with goal times and the Yoro red card](/images/liverpool-4-0-man-united-pochettino-debut-scoreline-card.jpg)
*Full time at Anfield: four Liverpool goals, one United red card.*

This was over almost before it started. Pochettino had four days in the job and three training sessions, the Evening Standard reported. His side lasted eight minutes with 11 players.

Liverpool's win is only their second league win at Anfield this season. Before kick-off they had scored just 4 home league goals. They doubled that in one evening.

For United, it is a ninth league game without a win. It also keeps their run of no clean sheets going.

## How the match unfolded

![Liverpool vs Man United match timeline: red card 8', goals 10', 13', 66', 82', United substitutions 22' and 46'](/images/liverpool-4-0-man-united-pochettino-debut-match-timeline.jpg)
*Key events from Liverpool vs Man United. Liverpool's xG was 4.38 to United's 0.63.*

**The red card (8').** Matthijs de Ligt misjudged a long ball over the top. Isak got in behind. Yoro came across and fouled him outside the box. Referee Sam Barrott judged Yoro to be the last man and showed a straight red. NBC Sports said Isak's touch was heavy and Senne Lammens might have reached the ball first, but that Yoro could have few complaints.

**Two goals in three minutes.** Szoboszlai drove the free-kick low past Lammens. Then Ngumoha beat Diogo Dalot on the left and cut the ball back. Wirtz finished with an improvised volley that went in off the bar.

**A quieter spell.** Pochettino took off Bryan Mbeumo for Harry Maguire after 22 minutes. United held on until half-time at 2-0.

**Liverpool finish the job.** Isak missed a clear chance just after the hour. On 66 minutes, Bradley Barcola left Lisandro Martínez behind and squared for Ngumoha to score. On 82 minutes, Virgil van Dijk won the ball high up, and Wirtz slipped in Muñoz, who smashed in the fourth.

## Pochettino's tactics: what he tried and what changed

![Tactical board: Liverpool 4-2-3-1 against Manchester United 3-4-2-1 starting line-ups](/images/liverpool-4-0-man-united-pochettino-debut-tactical-formation-board.jpg)
*Starting shapes. United switched to a 5-3-1 after Yoro's red card.*

Pochettino started with a back three: Yoro, De Ligt and Martínez. Dalot and Patrick Dorgu were the wing-backs. Bruno Fernandes and Mbeumo played behind Matheus Cunha.

The Daily Star noted that picking De Ligt ahead of Maguire raised questions before kick-off. That choice looked bad within minutes.

After the red card, he made these changes:
- **22':** Maguire on for Mbeumo. This Is Anfield described the new shape as 5-3-1.
- **46':** Joshua Zirkzee on for Cunha.
- **65':** Mason Mount and Noussair Mazraoui on for Youri Tielemans and Dorgu.
- **87':** Andrey Santos on for Kobbie Mainoo.

The 5-3-1 slowed the game for a while. But it could not stop Liverpool. They had 60 touches in United's box to United's 9.
