If you want to know the MLS scores today, the quickest answer is a live scores page that updates on its own. But there is a bit more to it than refreshing a page. Knowing how to read the score, the match status and the time makes following Major League Soccer much easier, especially when you live far from North America.

This guide explains where to find MLS scores, what the labels mean and a few habits that save time on match day.

## Where to find the MLS score today

The simplest place is the [MLS live scores today](https://kickpulse-football.vercel.app/mls-live-scores/) page on KickPulse. It lists every Major League Soccer match for the selected day, with the score, the match minute and the current status. The page refreshes by itself during matches, so you do not need to keep reloading it.

If you want a different day, use the date bar at the top. It shows the days around today, plus a calendar for any other date. That is handy when you have missed a game and want to check the result the next morning.

## What the labels on a match card mean

Match cards look simple, but each part tells you something.

- **A minute, such as 55':** the match is live and this is roughly how far it has gone.
- **HT:** half-time. The teams are in the dressing rooms and play resumes shortly.
- **FT:** full-time. The match is finished and the score is final.
- **A kickoff time:** the match has not started yet. KickPulse shows it in your own time zone.

The three tabs above the list, Live, Finished and Upcoming, filter the day for you. If you only care about what is happening right now, open Live. If you are catching up, open Finished.

## Live scores versus final scores

A live score is a snapshot. It can change in seconds, and in rare cases a goal is taken back after a video review, so a score you saw a moment ago may be corrected. A final score is different. Once a match shows FT, the result stands.

This matters when you share a score with friends. If a game is live, say it is "currently" 2-1. If it is finished, say it ended 2-1. It sounds like a small thing, but it avoids arguments.

## Tap a match for more detail

The score is only the start. Tap any match to open its details, where you can see events such as goals and cards, along with stats and lineups when they are available. If you are watching a game and want to confirm who scored or who was booked, this is faster than searching online.

## Following MLS from Pakistan, the UK or Europe

Most MLS matches are played in the North American evening, so for fans in Pakistan, the UK and Europe they are late at night or early morning. Two things help.

First, rely on a page that converts kickoff times for you. Our guide to [MLS kickoff times in Pakistan, the UK and Europe](https://kickpulse-final.vercel.app/articles/mls-kickoff-times-pakistan-uk-europe/) explains the maths if you want to do it yourself.

Second, check the results in the morning. Open the [MLS results today](https://kickpulse-football.vercel.app/mls-live-scores/) view, switch to Finished, and you will see everything you missed in one scroll.

## Follow one team

If you support a single club, you do not need the full list. Use the search box on KickPulse to type a team name. For Inter Miami fans, the [Inter Miami live score](https://kickpulse-football.vercel.app/inter-miami-live-score/) page goes straight to that club's matches.

## Before the match: read, then watch

Scores tell you what happened. They do not tell you why. For previews, team news and predicted lineups, read our blog, [Pitchside Notes](https://kickpulse-final.vercel.app/). A good starting point is the [MLS match preview checklist](https://kickpulse-final.vercel.app/articles/mls-match-preview-checklist/), which shows what to look for before kickoff.

## A simple match-day routine

1. Open the live scores page and check which matches are on today.
2. Read the team news before kickoff, and note who is missing.
3. Keep the Live tab open during the game.
4. After full-time, check the Finished tab and the league table to see how the result changes the standings.

With that routine, you will always know the MLS scores today without hunting for them.
