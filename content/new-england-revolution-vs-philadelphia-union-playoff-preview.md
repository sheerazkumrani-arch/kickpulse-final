## How the Revs and Union Got Here: Season Form Compared

![Season form comparison: New England 58 points (18 wins, 4 draws, 12 losses) against Philadelphia 50 points (14 wins, 8 draws, 12 losses), with the Revs conceding 47 goals and the Union scoring 66](/images/revs-union-r1g1-season-form.jpg)

New England finished third in the East on 58 points (18-12-4) and conceded just 47 goals, one of the best defensive records among playoff teams. They return to the postseason after a two-year wait, though they lost to Inter Miami and Orlando City just before it.

Philadelphia's story is wilder. The Union won once in their first 15 league games, then turned it around under interim head coach Ryan Richter, going 15 matches unbeaten, a club record. They finished sixth on 50 points (14-12-8) with 66 goals and beat Toronto FC 4–1 on Decision Day.

## Head-to-Head: Revs Swept the Union in 2026

![Revolution vs Union head-to-head: Revs won 2–1 at Gillette on May 9 and 2–1 at Subaru Park on October 24, while the Union won the 2023 Round One series 2–0](/images/revs-union-r1g1-head-to-head.jpg)

The Revolution won 2–1 at Gillette Stadium on May 9 and 2–1 at Subaru Park on October 24, a result that ended Philadelphia's unbeaten run. Before 2026, New England had lost six in a row to the Union.

Playoff history favours Philly. The Union won the 2023 Round One series 2–0 (3–1, then 1–0 at Gillette). This is the third first-round meeting in seven seasons, and the Revs carry a four-match postseason losing run into Game 1.

## Key Players to Watch

![Key players: Carles Gil 13 goals and 13 assists, Matt Turner 140 saves, Peyton Miller 7 goals and 9 assists for the Revolution; Cavan Sullivan 9 goals and 14 assists, Milan Iloski 19 goals, Bruno Damiani 13 goals for the Union](/images/revs-union-r1g1-key-players.jpg)

**Revolution:** Captain Carles Gil produced 13 goals and 13 assists and became the eighth player in MLS history to reach 100 regular-season assists. Goalkeeper Matt Turner made 140 saves, second in the league, and is a Goalkeeper of the Year finalist. Peyton Miller, 19, posted 7 goals and 9 assists and broke into the USMNT.

**Union:** Cavan Sullivan, 17, has 9 goals and 14 assists, a teenage MLS record of 23 goal contributions. Milan Iloski leads the team with 19 goals, and Bruno Damiani scored 12 of his 13 goals in the second half of the season.

## Tactics: Mitrović's Structure vs Richter's Union

Marko Mitrović's Revs line up in a nominal 4-3-3 that shifts shape in and out of possession. Turner sits behind a compact defence, and Gil pulls the strings in front. Mitrović calls it a "survival mode" mindset.

Philadelphia play with a back four and two forwards. Sullivan and Iloski create, Damiani finishes, and 6-foot-7 centre back Neil Pierre lets the Union defend aggressively. The Revs limited them to two goals across two games this season, so Gil's control against Union speed is the key battle.

## Best-of-3 Format, Game 2 and Game 3 Dates

![Series schedule: Game 1 on November 21 at Gillette Stadium, Game 2 on November 28 at Philadelphia, Game 3 on December 2 if needed, and MLS Cup on December 18](/images/revs-union-r1g1-series-schedule.jpg)

The first team to win two games advances. Game 2 is on November 28 in Philadelphia, and Game 3, if needed, is on December 2. There is no extra time: if a game is level after 90 minutes, a penalty shootout decides it. The winner meets Inter Miami (No. 2) or the Columbus Crew (No. 7) in the conference semifinals, with MLS Cup on December 18. The Revs are chasing their first MLS Cup appearance since 2014.
