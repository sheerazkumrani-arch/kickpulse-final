## Two records are on the line at the Emirates

![Arsenal vs Dortmund record-chase graphic: 16 unbeaten, club record 17, Liverpool's 13 straight home wins](/images/arsenal-vs-dortmund-prediction-kepa-record-record-chase.webp)
*Arsenal chase two records tonight. Source: Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026.*

Arsenal are 16 games unbeaten in the Champions League league phase and group stage. One more match without defeat equals the club record of 17, set between 2003 and 2006 (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). A win would also match Liverpool's English record of 13 straight home wins in this stage.

The surprising number is about goals, not defence. Arsenal have scored at least twice in 21 of their last 22 home games in this stage of the competition (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026).

The record chase matters for the table too. Arsenal sit third, two points behind Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid (Goal.com, 23 Nov 2026).

## Can Kepa Arrizabalaga keep the fortress intact?

![Stat card: Arsenal scored twice or more in 21 of their last 22 home Champions League group and league-phase games](/images/arsenal-vs-dortmund-prediction-kepa-record-home-scoring.webp)
*The surprising number. Source: Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026.*

Kepa Arrizabalaga can, if the defence in front of him stays narrow. Arsenal have conceded just two goals at home all season, per Sports Mole. The bigger risk is Dortmund, who have scored in every Champions League game.

Kepa's only previous Champions League start for Arsenal was the 3-2 win over Kairat Almaty in January. Mikel Arteta said Raya's injury does not look serious and needs no surgery (The Football Faithful, 24 Nov 2026). William Saliba is also in contention for his first start of the season, reports the Evening Standard [VERIFY final XI].

Four of Dortmund's last five matches had both teams scoring (Goal.com). Fabio Silva scored and assisted Karetsas in the 2-1 win over Real Betis.

## Arsenal vs Dortmund team news and probable lineups

![Arsenal and Borussia Dortmund injury list before the Champions League match](/images/arsenal-vs-dortmund-prediction-kepa-record-injury-board.webp)

Arsenal are without David Raya (shoulder), Bukayo Saka (quad) and Martin Zubimendi (hamstring). Dortmund miss Emre Can, Giannis Konstantelias, Samuele Inacio, Justin Lerma and Mussa Kaba (Sports Mole, 23 Nov 2026).

Serhou Guirassy (hamstring) and Carney Chukwuemeka are back in the Dortmund squad. Arsenal loanee Ethan Nwaneri is eligible but expected on the bench.

## Our Arsenal vs Dortmund prediction
Dortmund arrive unbeaten in eight games since losing 1-0 to Bodo/Glimt on 14 October, winning six (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). Yet they have no Champions League clean sheet this season. The head-to-head is tight: Arsenal lead four wins to three, and won the last competitive meeting 2-0 in November 2014.

**Prediction (opinion, not fact): Arsenal 2-1 Borussia Dortmund.**

Arsenal's scoring habit at home is the strongest trend in this fixture. Guirassy's return and Kepa's inexperience at this level make an away goal likely.
