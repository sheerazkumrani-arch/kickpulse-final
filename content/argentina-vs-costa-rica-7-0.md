# Argentina Bounce Back After Uruguay Loss with 7-0 Rout of Costa Rica

![Argentina vs Costa Rica 7-0 final score, international friendly, 17 November 2026](images/argentina-vs-costa-rica-7-0.jpg)

Argentina ended 2026 with a statement. Only four days after losing 2-1 to Uruguay, Lionel Scaloni's team crushed Costa Rica 7-0 in an international friendly at the Estadio Mario Alberto Kempes in Córdoba on 17 November 2026. Nico Paz, Julián Álvarez and Franco Mastantuono each scored twice, and Alexis Mac Allister added the seventh. It was a dominant answer to a rare defeat.

## Argentina vs Costa Rica 7-0: Final Score and Match Summary

The final score, Argentina 7-0 Costa Rica, left no room for debate. The match kicked off at 8 pm local time in front of a big crowd of home supporters, and Argentina were in complete control. Costa Rica arrived after a 2-0 defeat to Ecuador and could not slow down the hosts at any stage. The result was Argentina's second 7-0 victory since the start of October, after the same scoreline against Burkina Faso.

For Argentina, it was the perfect way to close the year. For Costa Rica, it was a painful night in a difficult run of results.

## How Argentina Bounced Back After the Uruguay Defeat

![Argentina bounce back after Uruguay defeat: 1-2 vs Uruguay, then 7-0 vs Costa Rica](images/argentina-bounce-back-after-uruguay-loss.jpg)

The Costa Rica game mattered because of what came before it. On 13 November, Argentina lost 2-1 to Uruguay, a result that raised questions about the team's form. Four days later, the answer came on the pitch.

Instead of showing nerves, Argentina played with speed, confidence and sharp finishing. A heavy win in the very next match is the clearest sign of a team that refuses to let one bad night become a bad run. This is why many fans are calling the 7-0 win a true bounce-back performance.

## Nico Paz and Mastantuono: Young Stars Shine

Two of the biggest talking points were Nico Paz and Franco Mastantuono. Both are among Argentina's brightest young attackers, and both scored twice. Their movement and creativity gave Costa Rica constant problems.

Julián Álvarez also delivered a double, proving once again how dangerous he is in front of goal. Together, the three forwards showed that Argentina's attack is full of talent for years to come. Mac Allister's goal from midfield was the final touch on a complete team display.

## Costa Rica's Struggles Continue

For Costa Rica, the trip to Argentina was always going to be tough. Just four days earlier they had lost 2-0 to Ecuador, and head coach Ruiz, who took the job permanently in October, will need time to rebuild confidence.

A 7-0 defeat is a heavy blow, but friendlies are meant for learning. Costa Rica will hope to fix their defensive problems before their next competitive matches.

## What the 7-0 Win Means for Argentina's Future

A friendly is not a trophy, but this win still matters. It shows Argentina's depth, its young talent and its ability to react after a setback. Scaloni now ends the year with a squad that is confident, balanced and full of goals.

For supporters, the message is simple: even in a quiet part of the calendar, this team plays to win and wants to win big.
