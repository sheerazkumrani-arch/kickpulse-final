## Slovan Bratislava's losing streak: how close is the record?

![Bar chart: Marseille 13 and Slovan Bratislava 12 consecutive Champions League defeats](/images/slovan-bratislava-vs-real-betis-prediction-champions-league-losing-streak-record.webp)
*One more loss equals Marseille's record. Source: UEFA.*

Slovan are one defeat from equalling the longest losing run in Champions League history. UEFA says the 4-0 loss to LASK on Matchday 4 made it 12 from 12, one short of the 13 Marseille lost between 2012 and 2020 (UEFA, 23 Nov 2026).

Slovan have trailed at half-time in 11 of those 12 games and conceded first in 11. Their 12 matches have produced 52 goals, 4.33 per game.

Slovan lost 6-1 to Paris Saint-Germain, then fell to Stuttgart, Roma and LASK (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). They have scored once and conceded 17. Only BATE Borisov, with 19 in 2014/15, conceded more across the first four games of a Champions League season (UEFA).

## Why does the first goal decide this match?

![First-goal infographic: Betis 4/4 wins when scoring first, Slovan conceded first in 11 of 12](/images/slovan-bratislava-vs-real-betis-prediction-champions-league-first-goal-factor.webp)
*Why the opening goal matters. Source: UEFA.*

The first goal is the swing factor because both teams have extreme records around it. Betis have won all four Champions League matches in which they scored first (UEFA, 23 Nov 2026). Slovan have conceded first in 11 of 12.

Manuel Pellegrini's Betis beat Lille 3-2, Porto 3-2 and Feyenoord 1-0 before losing 2-1 at Borussia Dortmund (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026).

One small positive: Yaya Touré switched from a 3-4-3 to a 4-1-4-1, and the change brought a draw and a win in their last two games Slovan are still winless in five at home.
