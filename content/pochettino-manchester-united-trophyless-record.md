# Can Mauricio Pochettino Save Manchester United Despite His Trophyless Record?

![Mauricio Pochettino at Manchester United: can a trophyless manager save the club?](images/pochettino-man-united-hero.jpg)

*Last updated: November 19, 2026. This article is updated after every Manchester United match. For live results, check [live Premier League scores on KickPulse](https://kickpulse-football.vercel.app/).*

**Quick answer:** Mauricio Pochettino has no major trophy from his spells at Tottenham and Chelsea, but Manchester United's immediate goal is survival, not silverware. United are 18th in the Premier League after one win in 10 games, their worst start in 40 years. Pochettino's strengths are rebuilding squads, high pressing and player development, which are exactly what a struggling team needs.

- **Start date:** November 18, 2026
- **Contract:** Until summer 2029

Manchester United have appointed Mauricio Pochettino to rescue their season. The Argentine signs a contract until the summer of 2029 after a reported £7.4m to £7.5m buyout from [U.S. Soccer](https://www.ussoccer.com/). He started on November 18, 2026, following the dismissal of Michael Carrick. He now faces the biggest challenge of his career, and a fair question from supporters: can a manager with no major trophy in England fix a club of this size?

## How Bad Is Manchester United's Start to the Season?

Manchester United have struggled to regain their former standing for over a decade, but this is a new low. They sit 18th in the [Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/), inside the relegation zone, with just one win in their opening 10 league matches. It is their worst start to a season in 40 years. You can follow the full table on our [Premier League live scores and standings page](https://kickpulse-football.vercel.app/).

Pochettino has moved quickly to reshape the staff. His long-time assistants Jesus Perez, Miguel D'Agostino, Toni Jimenez, Alec Scott and Sebastiano Pochettino have arrived. Michael Carrick, Jonny Evans, Steve Holland, Travis Binnion, Craig Mawson and Jonathan Woodgate have left.

## What Is Pochettino's Trophyless Record?

Pochettino's record is the main criticism of this appointment. He has managed 344 matches at Premier League "Big Six" clubs, including 293 with Tottenham Hotspur and a spell at Chelsea, without winning a major trophy. That is an unusually long run for a manager at that level.

![Mauricio Pochettino's career timeline from Espanyol to the USMNT, showing no trophies in England](images/pochettino-career-timeline.jpg)

His career has been full of near-misses:

- **Espanyol (2009 to 2012):** Built a competitive side but won no trophy.
- **Southampton (2013 to 2014):** Finished in the top half, with no trophy.
- **Tottenham Hotspur (2014 to 2019):** Reached the 2015 League Cup final and the 2019 [UEFA Champions League](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/) final, and finished Premier League runners-up, but won nothing.
- **Chelsea:** A spell in England that also ended without silverware.

## Did Pochettino Win Anything at PSG or with the USMNT?

At Paris Saint-Germain, Pochettino won Ligue 1 and the Coupe de France. Those were trophies PSG were widely expected to win given their domestic dominance, so critics give them less weight.

With the United States Men's National Team from 2024 to 2026, the pattern of near-misses continued. His team reached the 2025 Gold Cup final and the Round of 16 at the 2026 FIFA World Cup, but finished without a trophy. For a club whose identity is built on winning, hiring a serial runner-up is a real gamble.

## What Does Pochettino Need to Fix First at Manchester United?

Survival and stability come before trophies. Pochettino is known for high-intensity, high-pressing, attacking football. Current Premier League metrics show United ranking very low for total distance covered and sprint intensity, so closing that fitness gap mid-season will be essential if his system is to work.

## Final Verdict: Is Pochettino the Right Manager for Manchester United?

Manchester United do not need a trophy tomorrow. They need a manager who can stop the slide, rebuild fitness and lift the team out of the relegation zone. Pochettino has rebuilt struggling squads before, at Southampton and Tottenham. Whether he can finally shed the "trophyless" label at Old Trafford will define the next chapter of his career.
