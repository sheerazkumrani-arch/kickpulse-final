# Plzen 4-0 Benfica: Kabongo Double Ends the Leaders' Perfect Run

![Plzen 4-0 Benfica: Europa League matchday 5 result graphic with the final score and key numbers](/images/plzen-benfica-uel-md5-hero.jpg)

*Updated: 27 November 2026, 12:45 AM PKT*

**Executive summary:** Plzen 4-0 Benfica is the shock result of Europa League matchday 5. Benfica arrived at the Doosan Arena as the only side in the league phase with 12 points from 12. Viktoria Plzeň had three. By the 46th minute it was 4-0, with Patrik Hrošovský and Prince Adu on the scoresheet and Christophe Kabongo scoring twice.

## Key events timeline

![Plzen vs Benfica key events timeline: goals at 30, 43, 44 and 46 minutes and two yellow cards](/images/plzen-benfica-uel-md5-section-1-timeline.jpg)

- **30'** Hrošovský finishes from close range after a cut-back from Lukáš Červ. 1-0.
- **43'** Kabongo sets up Adu inside the box. 2-0.
- **44'** Kabongo cuts in and beats Anatoliy Trubin. 3-0.
- **46'** Plzeň counter straight from the restart; Hrošovský feeds Kabongo. 4-0.
- **46'** Georgiy Sudakov replaces Enzo Barrenechea for Benfica.
- **59'** Marco Silva makes a triple change: João Palhinha, Andreas Schjelderup and Claudio Echeverri come on.
- **70'** Plzeň bring on Denis Višinský, Stefan Pirgić and Tomáš Ladra.
- **75'** Matěj Vydra comes on for Plzeň.
- **77'** Adam Gabriel (Plzeň) is booked.
- **78'** Schjelderup (Benfica) is booked.
- **80'** Alexandr Sojka comes on for Plzeň.
- **83'** Jhon Durán comes on for Benfica.

## Plzen 4-0 Benfica: how the night turned

![Plzen 4-0 Benfica score card with Hrošovský, Adu and Kabongo goal minutes](/images/plzen-benfica-uel-md5-section-2-score-card.jpg)

For half an hour this looked like the match everyone expected. Benfica had the ball, Plzeň defended deep. Then they scored with almost their first clean move. Červ got free on the right and Hrošovský turned his cross in from inside the six-yard box.

What followed was the decisive spell of the game. Adu scored on 43 minutes, Kabongo a minute later, and Plzeň went in 3-0 up. The fourth came barely a minute after the restart: Hrošovský carried the ball forward on the break and Kabongo finished from the middle of the box.

Four goals in 16 minutes of football. Benfica never recovered, and the second half passed without another goal.

## Why Benfica were beaten

![First-half stats Plzen vs Benfica: possession 28-72, passes 134-357, shots on target 6-2, xG 0.82-0.30](/images/plzen-benfica-uel-md5-section-3-first-half-stats.jpg)

**The facts.** At half-time Benfica had 72% of the ball and completed far more passes (357 to 134, according to Vavel's live data). Plzeň had six shots on target to Benfica's two. Forza Football's first-half expected goals (xG) were 0.82 for Plzeň and 0.30 for Benfica.

**Our analysis.** Possession was not the problem; what Benfica did with it was. Plzeň, set up in a back five under Radoslav Kováč, let Benfica pass sideways and waited for the moment to break. When they won the ball, they went forward fast, and Benfica's back line was repeatedly caught short of numbers. Their finishing was excellent: 0.82 xG usually brings one goal, not three.

Benfica's attack also lacked bite. Vangelis Pavlidis and Dodi Lukébakio both had chances, but Benfica created little against a packed defence.

## Benfica's first defeat: what it means

![Benfica's five straight wins before their 4-0 defeat in Plzeň](/images/plzen-benfica-uel-md5-section-4-benfica-form.jpg)

Benfica had won their previous five matches in all competitions, including a 3-1 cup win at Torreense four days earlier, according to ESPN. This was their first defeat of the league phase, and it came after winning their first four games.

Silva's reaction told its own story. He changed four players by the hour mark and sent on Jhon Durán later, but the changes brought more of the ball, not more danger. The second half produced no more goals, only two bookings.

One bad night does not undo four wins, but four goals conceded raises a defensive question.

## What it means for the Europa League table

![Provisional Europa League table: Benfica 4th on 12 points, Plzeň 19th on 6 points](/images/plzen-benfica-uel-md5-section-5-europa-league-table.jpg)

Before kick-off, Plzeň had three points from one win and three defeats (a 1-0 win at Lillestrøm, and losses to Union Saint-Gilloise, Ferencváros and Levski Sofia). This win takes them to six points.

Benfica stay on 12. Sky Sports' table, updated while other matchday 5 games were still being played, showed Benfica dropping to fourth and Plzeň climbing to 19th. Those positions may change once the night's later games finish, so check the official UEFA table.

The format is simple. The top eight go straight to the last 16, and teams from ninth to 24th play knockout play-offs. With three matches left, Benfica are still well placed for the top eight. Plzeň are now inside the play-off places.

## What comes next

Matchday 6 is in December; check UEFA.com for each club's next opponent. Benfica's focus turns to fixing their defence away from home. For Plzeň, the job is to show this was more than one magical half.
