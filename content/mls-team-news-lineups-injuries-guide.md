Fans often ask where reliable MLS team news comes from. Here is a simple way to check it.

## Official club channels
Clubs publish injury and availability updates on their own websites and social accounts. These are the most reliable sources, so use them first.

## Pre-match press conferences
Coaches usually speak to the media a day or two before a match. They often say who is fit, who is doubtful and whether changes are likely.

## Confirmed lineups
Starting lineups are normally released roughly an hour before kickoff. Anything you read earlier is a prediction.

## Reading the signs of rotation
Teams that play several matches in a short time, or travel long distances, often change the lineup. Look at how many days of rest each side has had.

## A good habit
Write your own short note for each match: who is out, who is doubtful, and one change you expect. It is the fastest way to learn the league.
