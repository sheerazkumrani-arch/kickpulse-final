Then, in the **108th minute**, Polish striker **Adam Buksa** found the decisive goal, the strike that finally cracked Alcoyano's resistance and made it 2-1. The hosts had nothing left to answer with, and the tie was put beyond doubt late in extra time. **Adrián Fuentes** took the chance with a **left-footed shot from the centre of the box into the high centre of the goal**, **assisted by Pablo Torre**, to make it **Alcoyano 1, Mallorca 3**.
