# Norway vs England Nations League 2027: Can Haaland and Ødegaard Break England’s Hearts?

By **Sheraz Khan** · [Pitchside Notes](https://kickpulse-final.vercel.app/) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

![Norway vs England Nations League quarter-final 2027 hero banner with Haaland, Kane, 19 England goals and Norway's 96th-minute penalty](images/nor-eng-nl-2027-hero-banner.jpg)

Some teams simply arrive at a quarter-final. Norway fought for theirs, and England earned theirs the hard way. Now they meet again, only months after the World Cup quarter-final in Miami. Norway host the first leg on 25–27 March 2027 and England host the second on 28–30 March. The winner plays Belgium or Germany.

## Norway’s Copenhagen Comeback: How They Reached the Quarter-Finals

![Norway Copenhagen comeback against Denmark: 2–0 down inside 15 minutes, Ødegaard equalises, Haaland's 96th-minute penalty makes it 3–2](images/nor-eng-nl-2027-copenhagen-comeback.jpg)

This is Norway’s first Nations League quarter-final, and it almost slipped away. On the final matchday in Copenhagen, Denmark led 2–0 inside 15 minutes through Rasmus Højlund and Pierre-Emile Højbjerg. Norway needed a win. The dream looked finished.

Then Erling Haaland pulled one back. Martin Ødegaard equalised with about seven minutes left. VAR spotted a handball, and in the 96th minute Haaland stepped up and scored the penalty. 3–2. Both teams finished on nine points, and Norway went through on head-to-head.

It was their second 3–2 win over Denmark, after Oslo, where Haaland scored twice. Portugal, the group winners, beat Norway 2–1 in October, so this side is dangerous but not flawless. Ståle Solbakken’s team also reached the World Cup quarter-finals in their first appearance for 28 years.

## England’s Route: Kane’s Goals and a Madrid Statement

England’s road was calmer but no less proud. Thomas Tuchel’s team won the group with 15 points, beating Spain 3–2 in Madrid on 15 November to end their 43-match unbeaten run. They scored 19 goals, the most in the league phase.

Harry Kane scored in all six games and finished with eight goals, including doubles against Croatia and Czechia. He also became England’s most-capped player with 126 appearances. Jude Bellingham led the assists chart with six.

## World Cup Rematch: England 2–1 Norway in Miami

This tie carries scars. In Miami, England won 2–1 with two goals from Jude Bellingham after Andreas Schjelderup gave Norway the lead. Norway will feel they lost narrowly, and England know they were pushed. For anyone checking the Norway England head to head record, that is the latest chapter.

## Haaland vs Kane and Ødegaard vs Bellingham: The Key Battles

![Haaland vs Kane key battles: Haaland 4 goals in two Denmark wins, Kane 8 goals in 6 games, Ødegaard 5 assists, Bellingham 6 assists](images/nor-eng-nl-2027-key-battles.jpg)

The headline is Haaland vs England, but the midfield duel may matter more. Ødegaard creates most of Norway’s chances, and Bellingham does the same for England. Whoever controls that area feeds the strikers.

## Can Norway Upset England?

England are favourites on form and depth, but Norway have reasons to believe:

- **Haaland.** One chance is often enough, and he is Norway’s all-time record scorer.
- **Momentum.** They have shown they can recover from bad situations.
- **The first leg at home.** Keep it tight in Norway, and the tie stays open.

The risk is a slow start. Norway conceded twice in 15 minutes against Denmark, and England’s attack punishes that better.
