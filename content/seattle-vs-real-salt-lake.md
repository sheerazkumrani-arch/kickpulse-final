## 3. Team News and Injury Updates

Team availability could play a major role in deciding this Wild Card match.

Seattle Sounders have experienced defensive uncertainty, and one confirmed absence will be particularly important.

Defender Jackson Ragen has received a two-match suspension from the MLS Disciplinary Committee following an incident in the match against LAFC on November 7.

He will miss the Wild Card match against Real Salt Lake.

This is a significant absence because Ragen has been an important part of Seattle's defensive unit throughout the campaign.

Yeimar Gómez Andrade has also been dealing with a hamstring issue, although there have been indications that he could return. His availability should be checked before kickoff.

Seattle will still have important players available in other areas of the pitch. Cristian Roldan provides experience and energy in midfield, while Dejan Joveljić remains an important attacking option.

Real Salt Lake also have an important injury situation to consider.

Midfielder Diego Luna underwent meniscus repair surgery in September and was expected to miss the remainder of the 2026 season. His absence reduces the team's creative options.

However, Real Salt Lake have continued to find attacking solutions.

Sergi Solans has contributed 15 goals and four assists, making him one of the team's most important attacking players. Morgan Guilavogui is another forward who can provide movement and attacking support.

The final injury report should be checked before kickoff because player availability can change.
