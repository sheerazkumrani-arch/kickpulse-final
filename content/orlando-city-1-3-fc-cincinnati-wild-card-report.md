# Orlando City 1-3 FC Cincinnati: Evander Steals Wild Card

![Orlando City 1-3 FC Cincinnati MLS Wild Card banner: Evander two goals and one assist, 21 shots to 9, Samuel Gidi 85th-minute goal](/images/orlando-city-1-3-fc-cincinnati-hero-banner.jpg)

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

FC Cincinnati beat Orlando City 3-1 in the MLS Cup Playoffs Wild Card match at Inter&Co Stadium on Thursday 19 November 2026. Martín Ojeda put Orlando ahead in the 26th minute, but Evander equalised from the penalty spot, scored again early in the second half and set up Samuel Gidi's late third goal. Cincinnati now face Supporters' Shield winners Nashville SC in Round One, and Orlando City are out.

## Orlando City vs FC Cincinnati Result: Key Facts

![Orlando City 1-3 FC Cincinnati goal timeline: Ojeda 26', Evander 43' penalty, Evander 50', Gidi 85', half-time 1-1](/images/orlando-city-1-3-fc-cincinnati-goals-timeline.jpg)

- **Final score:** Orlando City 1-3 FC Cincinnati
- **Competition:** MLS Cup Playoffs, Eastern Conference Wild Card (single game)
- **Date and venue:** Thursday 19 November 2026, Inter&Co Stadium, Orlando
- **Kick-off:** 7:30 pm ET (5:30 am in Pakistan on Friday)
- **Orlando City scorer:** Martín Ojeda 26'
- **FC Cincinnati scorers:** Evander 43' (penalty), Evander 50', Samuel Gidi 85'
- **Half-time:** Orlando City 1-1 FC Cincinnati

## Martín Ojeda Puts Orlando City Ahead

Orlando started the Wild Card night the way their fans wanted. Midfielder Martín Ojeda, the club's all-time leading scorer, finished in the 26th minute after an assist from Iván Angulo to make it 1-0. Orlando had 57% of the ball in the early stages, and 53% by full time.

That lead lasted only 17 minutes.

## Evander's Penalty Changes Everything

In the 43rd minute, Evander stepped up from the penalty spot and levelled the match at 1-1. Evander finished the regular season with 14 goals and 17 assists, and in May he had a hand in five goals in Cincinnati's 6-2 win over Orlando.

## Evander Strikes Again: Cincinnati Take the Lead

Cincinnati did not wait long after half-time. Within minutes of the restart, Evander scored again, this time after an assist from Tom Barlow, to make it 2-1. Live match reports differ by a minute on the exact time (49' or 50'), but either way Evander turned the game around with two goals in about seven minutes, one just before the break and one just after it.

## Samuel Gidi Seals the Win

With Orlando pushing for an equaliser, Cincinnati struck again in the 85th minute. Samuel Gidi scored the third, and the assist came from Evander, who finished with two goals and one assist. The win was sealed.

## What Next? FC Cincinnati vs Nashville SC

![FC Cincinnati vs Nashville SC Round One best-of-three: Game 1 Sunday 22 November, 4:00 pm ET at Geodis Park](/images/orlando-city-1-3-fc-cincinnati-nashville-round-one-schedule.jpg)

Cincinnati's reward is a tough one: a Round One best-of-three series against Nashville SC, the Supporters' Shield winners and the top seed in the East. Game 1 is on Sunday 22 November at Geodis Park in Nashville at 4:00 pm ET (2:00 am on Monday in Pakistan). Cincinnati lost 2-0 at Nashville on the final day of the regular season, so they know how hard the task is.

## Orlando City Out: Season Over After Wild Card Exit

For Orlando, the season ends in the first playoff game for the second year in a row. Last year they lost 3-1 at Chicago in the Wild Card match. This year, Orlando needed a Decision Day win just to earn the home game, and it was not enough. Orlando scored 63 goals in the regular season, but conceded 78.
