Football has a remarkable way of bringing even its greatest players back down to earth. Just when Inter Miami appeared ready to defend their MLS Cup crown, Columbus Crew delivered a painful reality check. A 3–0 home defeat has left Lionel Messi and his teammates fighting for playoff survival.

The Herons are not eliminated yet, but another defeat will bring their championship ambitions to an abrupt end. Messi has inspired remarkable recoveries before, although individual brilliance alone may not be enough this time.

## 1. Why Inter Miami face a must-win situation

![Best-of-three playoff format showing Miami must win Game 2 and Game 3](/images/inter-miami-elimination-messi-columbus-crew-2026-playoff.jpg)

Inter Miami travel to Columbus for Game 2 on November 27 at 8 PM ET (November 28 at 6 AM in Pakistan). The opening defeat leaves Miami trailing the best-of-three series 1–0.

Fortunately for the defending champions, the MLS first-round playoff format does not use aggregate scores. Miami simply need to win Game 2 before winning the deciding third match. Even a narrow 1–0 victory would keep their campaign alive. If the match finishes level after 90 minutes, penalties will determine the winner.

That distinction matters tactically. Miami must manage the occasion rather than desperately chase three goals that the competition format does not require. Columbus can afford to be patient, but Miami cannot afford another defeat.

## 2. Inter Miami comeback history: Three reasons for hope

![Three notable Inter Miami knockout recoveries against LAFC, Orlando and Nashville in 2025](/images/inter-miami-elimination-messi-columbus-crew-2026-comebacks.jpg)

Miami have survived several difficult knockout situations during the Messi era. Against LAFC in the 2025 Concacaf Champions Cup quarterfinals, Miami found themselves trailing 2–0 on aggregate. Messi scored twice as the Herons recovered to win 3–2 overall.

Another dramatic escape came against Orlando City in the 2025 Leagues Cup semifinal. Miami trailed before responding with three goals, including a Messi double, to secure a place in the final.

Perhaps the most relevant example came against Nashville during the 2025 MLS playoffs. Facing elimination in Game 3, Miami produced a commanding 4–0 victory, with Messi scoring twice and providing two assists.

These are three notable examples, rather than an exhaustive count of every comeback. Their 2024 elimination against Atlanta United also remains a reminder that superior individual talent does not guarantee playoff survival.

## 3. Inter Miami vs Columbus Crew: Strengths and weaknesses

![Comparison of Miami and Columbus attacking strengths and defensive vulnerabilities](/images/inter-miami-elimination-messi-columbus-crew-2026-comparison.jpg)

Inter Miami’s greatest advantage remains their attacking quality. Messi’s vision and ability to find space between defensive lines give the Herons a route back into almost any match. Quick passing combinations can turn apparently harmless possession into a clear scoring opportunity.

However, Miami’s defensive vulnerability is a concern. When players commit forward, the space left behind can expose the back line. Dependence on Messi to unlock organized opponents can also make their attacks predictable.

Columbus, meanwhile, enter the contest with confidence from their opening victory. Collective organization and attacking transitions could make them especially dangerous against a team that needs to win.

Their potential weakness lies in the space Miami could exploit when Columbus commit players forward. Quick combinations around Messi may disrupt their defensive structure, particularly if Miami score first. These are tactical assessments, not confirmed coaching plans.

## 4. Tactical analysis: How can Messi save Inter Miami?

![Three tactical priorities for Miami: midfield control, creative freedom and counterattack protection](/images/inter-miami-elimination-messi-columbus-crew-2026-tactics.jpg)

Miami should resist the temptation to attack recklessly. Their priority must be maintaining defensive balance while giving Messi freedom between the opposition’s midfield and defensive lines.

A compact midfield could help control transitions and protect the back four. Maintaining attacking width would also create opportunities for Messi to receive possession in central areas. The players around him must offer passing options rather than simply waiting for him to produce something extraordinary.

Columbus may attempt to exploit defensive gaps, particularly when Miami push their full-backs forward. The Herons must therefore improve their positioning immediately after losing possession.

Discipline will be equally important. The objective is not necessarily to produce spectacular football. A controlled, intelligent performance could prove more valuable than an unnecessarily aggressive attacking display.

## 5. Match prediction: Will Inter Miami avoid elimination?

![Game 2 outcomes: Miami victory forces Game 3, draw leads to penalties, defeat means elimination](/images/inter-miami-elimination-messi-columbus-crew-2026-scenarios.jpg)

Columbus deserve to enter Game 2 as favourites given their home advantage and the psychological benefit of their opening victory.

Miami, however, possess players capable of changing a match in seconds. An early goal could shift the pressure onto Columbus and completely transform the contest. A narrow Miami victory remains a realistic possibility, provided their defensive performance improves considerably.

**Prediction: Columbus Crew 1–2 Inter Miami.** This is an editorial forecast, not a confirmed result.

Ultimately, this match represents more than playoff survival. It is a test of Miami’s character, tactical maturity and championship mentality. Messi has inspired remarkable recoveries before, but this time, the entire team must rise to the occasion.

Great teams are not defined exclusively by their victories. They are also defined by how they respond when everything appears to be slipping away.
