# Como vs AEK Athens Prediction: AEK's 34-Year Away Curse

> **Quick Answer:** Como host AEK Athens in Champions League Matchday 5 at Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia on Tuesday 24 November 2026, 21:00 CET (01:00 AM PKT, Wednesday 25 November). The key fact: AEK have never won a Champions League away game. Our Como vs AEK Athens prediction: Como 3-1, an opinion, not a certainty.

My Como vs AEK Athens prediction starts with one number: 17. That is how many Champions League away matches AEK Athens have played since 1992 without a single win, with 12 defeats and five draws (Sports Mole, data as of 24 Nov 2026). Now they visit the competition's hottest debutant.

## Why is this match bigger than it looks?

Como sit 4th in the league phase with nine points, inside the automatic last-16 places, while AEK Athens are 29th with three points, in the elimination zone (Sofascore, data as of 24 Nov 2026). A Como win strengthens a top-eight push; an AEK defeat leaves them chasing miracles.

## Can AEK Athens finally break their away curse?

History says no, and this season says it louder. AEK lost 3-1 at Shakhtar Donetsk and 5-1 at Manchester City, conceding eight goals on two trips (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). Their record in Italy is just as thin: one win in 10 visits, against Fiorentina in last season's Conference League.

AEK play a direct 4-4-2 with only 40.5% average possession, built on long balls and transitions (Sofascore, 24 Nov 2026). That style needs clinical finishing, and AEK have missed 10 of their 12 big chances.

One warning: AEK led Real Madrid twice in Athens before losing 3-2 to a stoppage-time Federico Valverde goal (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). Cesc Fàbregas called AEK strong, physical and mentally tough (Como 1907 official site, 23 Nov 2026).

## Como's form: numbers no debutant should have

Como had never played a UEFA match before September, yet they have scored 19 goals in four Champions League games (Sofascore, 24 Nov 2026). They beat RB Leipzig 4-1 and Manchester United 4-1 at home, lost 3-2 at Feyenoord, then crushed Lens 9-1 away. They have scored first in all four games.

## Douvikas vs his own country: the hidden subplot

Greek striker Anastasios Douvikas is the competition's top scorer with six goals, one short of Vangelis Pavlidis's Greek record for a single Champions League campaign (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). Nico Paz adds three goals and five assists; his eight goal involvements are the best by a player at an Italian club after four games since Samuel Eto'o's 10 for Inter.

## Como vs AEK Athens team news and lineups

Como are without Moise Kean, Assane Diao, Jayden Addai and Jesús Rodríguez, all injured (Sofascore and Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026). Maxence Caqueret is doubtful [VERIFY].

AEK Athens lose Aboubakary Koita to a two-match ban after his red card against Real Madrid. Oleksandr Zubkov is injured. Kaan Kairinen and Kervin Arriaga are listed out by Sofascore [VERIFY], and Thomas Strakosha's fitness is unclear, with Alessandro Brignoli likely in goal [VERIFY].

## Como vs AEK Athens prediction: our score

Como's 90.1% passing accuracy should pin AEK deep for long spells. Still, Como have zero clean sheets in Europe, and AEK's aerial power from set pieces (Rǎzvan Marin's free kicks, Stavros Pilios's runs) can produce a goal.
