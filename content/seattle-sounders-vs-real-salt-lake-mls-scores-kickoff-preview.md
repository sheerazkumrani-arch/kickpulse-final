## Two Teams, Nothing Separating Them

![MLS scores form table: Seattle 46 points (12W 10D 12L, 8th) vs Real Salt Lake 45 points (13W 6D 15L, 9th), last five games 1W 2D 2L vs 4W 0D 1L, with 2026 meetings RSL 2–1 and SEA 2–0](/images/sea-rsl-wildcard-2026-form-table.jpg)

Seattle finished eighth in the West with 46 points (12 wins, 10 draws, 12 losses). Real Salt Lake sit one place and one point behind on 45 (13 wins, 6 draws, 15 losses). On paper, they're twins. In form, they're not.

- **Real Salt Lake:** four wins and one loss in their last five, scoring 2.2 goals per game
- **Seattle:** one win, two draws and two losses over the same stretch

But Lumen Field has a habit of rescuing Seattle. The Sounders are unbeaten in their last four home meetings with RSL (three wins, one draw) since March 2023. This season, RSL won 2–1 at home on February 28, then Seattle hit back with a 2–0 win on September 23, thanks to second-half goals from Danny Musovski and Albert Rusnák. Anyone who followed the MLS scoreboard that night saw how fast this rivalry can turn.

## Old Friends, New Colours

Few playoff ties come with this much personal history. Albert Rusnák now runs Seattle's midfield after five seasons at RSL (146 appearances, 42 goals). On the other side, DeAndre Yedlin wears RSL colours after coming through Seattle's academy. Expect plenty of emotion, and a few friendly words, before the whistle.
