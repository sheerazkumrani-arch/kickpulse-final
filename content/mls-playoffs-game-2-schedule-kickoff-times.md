## Why this weekend matters

The best-of-three format leaves no room to recover. Lose Game 1, lose Game 2, and the season is over. After Game 1, two of the biggest names in the league are in exactly that position.

Inter Miami, the defending MLS Cup champions and the No. 2 seed in the East, lost 3-0 at home to No. 7 Columbus after Gonzalo Luján was sent off in the 36th minute. Nashville SC, the Supporters' Shield winners, lost 1-0 at home to No. 9 FC Cincinnati, a team that finished 31 points behind them in the regular season.

So the top two seeds in the Eastern Conference have to win away from home just to force a Game 3. That is the main storyline of the weekend, but it is not the only one.

## How the Round One series work

Each Round One series is best of three. A win earns the series point, and a draw goes straight to a penalty shootout, so every game has a winner. The higher seed hosts Game 1 and, if needed, Game 3. The lower seed hosts Game 2.

That is why so many Game 2s are on the road for the leaders. If the trailing team wins on Saturday, the series goes to Game 3. Inter Miami vs Columbus Game 3 is already set for Wednesday 2 December at 7:30 PM ET (Thursday 3 December, 5:30 AM in Pakistan). According to the KickPulse fixture feed, New England vs Philadelphia would be played at 7:00 PM ET the same evening.

## Eastern Conference: can Miami and Nashville survive?

**Columbus vs Inter Miami** is the game most people will search for. Columbus scored a penalty through Gonzalo Tapia in the 9th minute of Game 1, then added two more goals as the game opened up against ten men. Miami now play at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field needing a win. Our view: Miami have the talent to turn it around, but Columbus have shown they can control the ball against them. Treat that as analysis, not a prediction.

**Cincinnati vs Nashville** is the bigger shock on paper. Nashville were the best team in the regular season by a distance, with 77 points and a +46 goal difference in the KickPulse table. One bad afternoon at GEODIS Park has put all of that at risk.

**Charlotte vs Chicago** follows a dramatic opener. Pep Biel scored in the 12th minute of stoppage time to give Charlotte a 2-1 away win, so Chicago, the higher seed, also has to win away.

**Philadelphia vs New England** is a different story. New England, the higher seed, won 2-0 at home and can close the series at Subaru Park.

## What to watch for

- **Two-game sweeps:** if all eight leaders win, every series ends this weekend and the Conference Semifinals line-up is set early.
- **Penalty shootouts:** a draw goes straight to penalties, so late-game drama is built into every match.
- **Time zone traps:** the Saturday games run from midday to late night in the US, which means the early hours of Sunday in the UK and Pakistan.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Inter Miami vs Columbus Game 2?
Game 2 is on Friday 27 November 2026 at 8:00 PM ET at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field in Columbus. That is 1:00 AM on Saturday in the UK and 6:00 AM on Saturday in Pakistan. Columbus lead the series 1-0.

### Are there MLS games tonight?
Yes. On Friday night US time, two Game 2s are played: Columbus vs Inter Miami (8:00 PM ET) and Real Salt Lake vs Vancouver (10:00 PM ET). Six more follow on Saturday 28 November.

### What happens if Inter Miami lose Game 2?
Inter Miami would be eliminated from the 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs, because Columbus would have won the best-of-three series 2-0. A Miami win forces Game 3 in Miami on Wednesday 2 December at 7:30 PM ET.

### What happens if an MLS playoff Game 2 ends in a draw?
There is no extra time in Round One. A drawn game goes straight to a penalty shootout, and the shootout winner takes the game and the series point.

### What time are MLS games in Pakistan?
Most MLS evening kickoffs fall between early morning and breakfast time in Pakistan. In winter, Pakistan is 10 hours ahead of US Eastern time, so an 8:00 PM ET kickoff is 6:00 AM PKT the next day.
