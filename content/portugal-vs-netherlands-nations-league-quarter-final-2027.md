# Can Portugal Win Without Ronaldo? Netherlands vs Portugal, the Nations League Tie With Everything on It

![Can Portugal win without Ronaldo? Netherlands vs Portugal Nations League quarter-final 2027 banner with four stats: 146 Ronaldo international goals, ten goals conceded each in six league games, 14 v 13 goals scored, and two legs on 27 and 30 March](/images/por-ned-nations-league-qf-2027-hero-banner.jpg)

**Short answer:** Portugal can compete without Cristiano Ronaldo, but the evidence so far is mixed. Ronaldo retired on 5 November, and Jorge Jesus's side now meet Xavi's Netherlands in the Nations League quarter-finals: first leg Saturday 27 March 2027 in the Netherlands, return leg Tuesday 30 March in Portugal, both at 20:45 CET/CEST.

Nobody could have scripted this. Fifteen days after Ronaldo walked away from Portugal following a row with his own coach, the draw in Nyon handed Jesus the one opponent that knows exactly what it feels like to lose a Nations League final to them. Portugal arrive as holders, with a new coach and no Ronaldo. The Netherlands arrive with a new coach and a fresh bruise. ## Portugal after Ronaldo: a team forced to grow up

![Portugal attack without Ronaldo: his 23 years, 234 caps and 146 goals, Gonçalo Ramos and João Félix with three league-phase goals each, plus the 1-4 defeat to Denmark and 3-2 win in Wales](/images/por-ned-nations-league-qf-2027-portugal-after-ronaldo.jpg)

Ronaldo's farewell was not quiet. He left after 23 years, 234 caps and a world-record 146 goals, and the split with Jesus was public. The coach says he asked him to stay. Jesus took over in the summer, so his first big test is life after a player who defined Portugal for two decades.

### Portugal's new attacking options in 2027

So who scores now? Gonçalo Ramos and João Félix, with three league-phase goals each. Spreading the load is healthier than hunting for one replacement, but call it what it is: Portugal's new generation of football is still being built, and both forwards are established names, not newcomers.

### The Denmark warning

Portugal won Group A4 and still conceded ten goals in six games. The sharpest moment came on 14 November, nine days after the retirement: a 4-1 home defeat to Denmark. Three days later they won 3-2 in Wales. Brilliant one week, shaky the next.

## Netherlands weaknesses after the Germany defeat: panic or one bad night?

![Netherlands weaknesses after the Germany defeat: a 7-0 win over Greece on 13 November and a 5-0 loss to Germany on 16 November, with Reijnders on four goals, Van Dijk on 100 caps and second place in Group A2](/images/por-ned-nations-league-qf-2027-netherlands-germany-defeat.jpg)

Three days. That is how long it took the Netherlands to go from beating Greece 7-0 to losing 5-0 in Germany. It was Xavi's first defeat as coach, and it had a price: second place in the group, which meant drawing a group winner, Portugal.

Before that night, Xavi's side had not lost, and the 7-0 against Greece proved they can score in bunches. Tijjani Reijnders hit a hat-trick against Greece and finished as top scorer with four, while captain Virgil van Dijk reached 100 caps. One heavy loss does not make a crisis, but Xavi now has to prove it.

## Portugal vs Netherlands 2019 final comparison: old wounds

![Portugal vs Netherlands 2019 final comparison: Portugal won the first Nations League final 1-0 in Porto, with Portugal winners in 2018/19 and 2024/25 and the Netherlands runners-up in 2018/19](/images/por-ned-nations-league-qf-2027-2019-final.jpg)

These two have been here before. Portugal won the first Nations League final 1-0 in Porto, through Gonçalo Guedes, and the Netherlands remain runners-up from that edition. Portugal later added the 2024/25 title.

The squads have changed. What has not changed is the mood: Portugal know how to win this competition, the Netherlands are still chasing their first trophy.
