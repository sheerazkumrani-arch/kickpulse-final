# Real Salt Lake Beat Seattle Sounders 4–2 on Penalties After 1–1 Thriller: MLS Playoff Match Report

![Real Salt Lake beat Seattle Sounders 4–2 on penalties after a 1–1 draw at Lumen Field in the 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs Wild Card, with RSL through to Round One and Seattle out](/images/rsl-sounders-penalties-hero-banner.jpg)

**Quick answer:** Real Salt Lake beat Seattle Sounders 4–2 in a penalty shootout after a 1–1 draw at Lumen Field in the 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs Western Conference Wild Card. Albert Rusnák scored for Seattle in the 36th minute, Morgan Guilavogui equalised for RSL in the 63rd, and Real Salt Lake scored all four of their penalties to advance to Round One. Seattle's season is over.

**Match facts**

- **Competition:** MLS Cup Playoffs 2026, Western Conference Wild Card
- **Venue:** Lumen Field, Seattle, Washington
- **Final score:** Seattle Sounders 1–1 Real Salt Lake (RSL win 4–2 on penalties)
- **Goals:** Rusnák 36' (SEA), Guilavogui 63' (RSL)
- **Kickoff:** 7:00 PM PT, Thursday, November 19 (8:00 AM PKT, Friday)
- **TV:** Apple TV

## MLS Score Today: Seattle 1–1 Real Salt Lake, RSL Win on Penalties

Seattle had the ball, the shots, the corners and the home crowd. Real Salt Lake had a goalkeeper who made five saves and a team that refused to break. When the final whistle went at 1–1, the MLS scoreboard didn't tell the story. A shootout would decide who lived and who went home, and it was RSL who came out on top.

If you checked the MLS score during the night, you saw a match that kept changing shape, and it ended in the cruellest way a knockout tie can.

## Rusnák's Penalty Drama: Saved at 31', Scored at 36'

![Match timeline of the first half: Albert Rusnák's penalty saved at 31', yellow card for Dominik Marczuk at 34', Rusnák's goal at 36', yellow cards for Morgan Guilavogui at 41' and Kim Kee-hee at 45'+2'](/images/rsl-sounders-penalties-first-half-timeline.jpg)

The biggest swing of the first half involved a man who knows both clubs well. On 31 minutes, Albert Rusnák, who spent five seasons at Real Salt Lake, stepped up to a penalty and saw it saved. Five minutes later, on 36 minutes, he put Seattle in front.

Few players go from the lowest moment to the best one that fast. The goal gave Lumen Field something to hold on to. It also set up a second half in which Seattle needed only to protect their lead.

Cards followed. Dominik Marczuk (34') and Morgan Guilavogui (41') were booked for Real Salt Lake. Kim Kee-hee (45'+2') went into the book for Seattle just before the break.

## Guilavogui Answers: Real Salt Lake Level at 63'

![Second-half and stoppage-time timeline: Morgan Guilavogui equalises for Real Salt Lake at 63', with Cristian Roldan booked at 76' and substitutions for both teams from 45' to 90'+12'](/images/rsl-sounders-penalties-second-half-timeline.jpg)

After the break, Real Salt Lake's bench got busy, with Zach Booth (45') and Lukas Engel (60') both featuring in the early changes. The reply came at 63 minutes, when Guilavogui scored. It was a goal worth plenty, because it dragged Seattle back to level ground and put the pressure on the home side.

Cristian Roldan was booked on 76 minutes, and both benches kept working. The match feed logged substitutions involving Kalani Kossa-Rienzi and Paul Arriola (71') and Snyder Brunell (86') for Seattle, and Samuel Junqua (77'), Ariath Piol (78') and Aiden Hezarkhani (85') for RSL. Stoppage time brought more: Paul Rothrock and Peter Kingston (90'+4') and Yeimar Gómez (90'+12') for Seattle, and DeAndre Yedlin (90'+6') for Real Salt Lake, the defender who came through Seattle's academy.

## Penalty Shootout: Real Salt Lake Win 4–2

![Penalty shootout result: Real Salt Lake win 4–2 with Solans, Engel, Hezarkhani and Quinton all scoring, while Seattle's Joveljić and Rusnák missed and Rothrock and Brunell scored](/images/rsl-sounders-penalties-shootout.jpg)

Seattle kicked first, and the shootout went wrong almost at once. Dejan Joveljić missed the opener, and Sergi Solans scored to put Real Salt Lake 1–0 up. Then came the cruellest moment of the night for Albert Rusnák. The man who had scored in the first half, and had a penalty saved earlier, stepped up and missed again. Lukas Engel made it 2–0 to RSL.

Seattle fought back. Paul Rothrock scored (1–2), but Aiden Hezarkhani answered (1–3). Snyder Brunell kept the Sounders alive (2–3), and then Philip Quinton made no mistake (2–4). Real Salt Lake's fourth kick left Seattle out of reach.

The shootout finished 4–2 to Real Salt Lake. Seattle, who had dominated so much of the 90 minutes, went out without losing in open play. For RSL, it was a classic knockout escape: four kicks, four goals, and a place in Round One.
