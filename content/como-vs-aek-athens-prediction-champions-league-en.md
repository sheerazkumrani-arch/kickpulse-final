## Is Como's scoring run sustainable?

![Comparison table of Como and AEK Athens Champions League points and goals](/images/como-vs-aek-athens-prediction-champions-league-ucl-table-snapshot.webp)
*League-phase standing before matchday five.*

Partly, but not at this rate. A gap this big over xG usually narrows over a season. Sofascore counts 23 big chances and 31 shots on target from 54 attempts in four games (data as of 24 Nov 2026).

Nico Paz is the engine. He has three goals and five assists, and Sports Mole notes his eight goal involvements are the best by a player from an Italian club after four games since Samuel Eto'o for Inter Milan. Tasos Douvikas leads the competition with six goals (Sports Mole, 24 Nov 2026).

Como's weakness: zero clean sheets in Europe despite conceding only six. AEK Athens win 59.42% of aerial duels, so long diagonals and set pieces are their best route. Răzvan Marin has already scored a direct free kick this campaign (Sofascore, 24 Nov 2026).

AEK Athens also waste chances: 12 big chances created, 10 missed. If Marko Nikolić's side finish better, Como could be tested.

Cesc Fàbregas said at his pre-match press conference that Como would stick to their ideas, calling AEK Athens a physical, dominant side that had troubled Real Madrid (Goal, 23–24 Nov 2026).

## Who is injured for Como and AEK Athens?

![List of injured and suspended players for Como and AEK Athens](/images/como-vs-aek-athens-prediction-champions-league-absentees.webp)
*Team news as of 24 Nov 2026; confirm with official team sheets.*

Como are without Moise Kean, Assane Diao, Jayden Addai and Jesús Rodríguez. Maxence Caqueret is listed as doubtful (Sofascore, 24 Nov 2026).

AEK Athens lose Aboubakary Koita to suspension after his red card against Real Madrid. Kervin Arriaga (collarbone), Kaan Kairinen and Oleksandr Zubkov are listed out by Sofascore. Barnabás Varga is back from injury and could start beside Luka Jović.

Our prediction is Como 3-1 AEK Athens. This is opinion, not fact. Como have scored first in seven of their last nine games, per Sofascore. AEK's away record and Koita's absence point one way. Expect AEK to score from a set piece, because Como have not kept a clean sheet in Europe.

## FAQ

**What time is Como vs AEK Athens in Pakistan?**
The match starts at 01:00 AM PKT on Wednesday 25 November 2026. Local time in Como is 21:00 CET.

**Is Como vs AEK Athens in the Champions League or Conference League?**
It is a UEFA Champions League league-phase game, matchday five. Como are in their first UEFA campaign.

**Have Como and AEK Athens played before?**
No competitive meeting is on record. Como had never played in a UEFA competition before September 2026, per Sports Mole.

**Who is Como's top scorer in the Champions League?**
Tasos Douvikas has six goals, the most in the competition. Nico Paz has three goals and five assists.

**Is Aboubakary Koita playing against Como?**
No. Aboubakary Koita is suspended after his red card against Real Madrid. Sports Mole reports a two-match ban.
