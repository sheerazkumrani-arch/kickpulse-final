A good MLS preview saves you time. Before you settle in for a match, check that the preview covers these six points.

## 1. Kickoff time in your own time zone
MLS clubs play across several North American time zones, so a kickoff can look strange from Europe or South Asia. Always convert the time before you plan your evening.

## 2. Recent form
Look at the last five results for both teams, home and away. One win after a bad run tells you less than a steady pattern.

## 3. Team news and injuries
Injuries and suspensions change a match more than almost anything else. The best previews name the missing players and say what difference it makes.

## 4. Predicted lineups
A predicted lineup is an educated guess, not a fact. Official lineups are usually announced about an hour before kickoff, so check again then.

## 5. The key matchup
Pick one battle that could decide the game, such as a fast winger against a slow full-back.

## 6. Where to watch
Broadcast rights differ by country. Check the league's official site for your region.

## About predictions
We sometimes give a score prediction. It is an opinion for fun, based on form and team news, and it is not betting advice.
