# Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan: Hutchinson Inspires a Wild Europa League Comeback

![Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan result graphic: Milan came from 2-0 down in Europa League matchday 5](/images/olympiacos-milan-uel-md5-hero.jpg)

*Updated: 27 November 2026, 1:15 AM PKT*

**Executive summary:** Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan is one of the comebacks of the Europa League season. Milan were 2-0 down after 49 minutes in Piraeus, following a Mike Maignan own goal and a Jota Silva finish. They then scored four times between the 56th minute and stoppage time. Omari Hutchinson scored one and set up two. Alphadjo Cissè, Christian Pulisic and Diego Moreira got the others. It was Olympiacos' first defeat of the league phase.

## Key events timeline

![Olympiacos vs AC Milan goals timeline: 31, 49, 56, 63, 71 and 90+5 minutes](/images/olympiacos-milan-uel-md5-section-1-timeline.jpg)

- **31'** Maignan own goal. 1-0 Olympiacos.
- **43'** Koni De Winter and Jota Silva are booked.
- **45'** Milan bring on Yunus Musah and Pulisic at half-time.
- **49'** Jota Silva scores. 2-0 Olympiacos.
- **56'** Hutchinson scores. 2-1.
- **63'** Cissè heads in from Hutchinson's cross. 2-2.
- **71'** Pulisic volleys in from Hutchinson's cross. 2-3.
- **90+5'** Moreira finishes a counter-attack after Pulisic's pass. 2-4.

## Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan: how the match turned

![Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan score card with every goal and minute](/images/olympiacos-milan-uel-md5-section-2-score-card.jpg)

For almost an hour, this was Olympiacos' night. They went ahead on 31 minutes when Maignan turned the ball into his own net, and Jota Silva doubled the lead four minutes after the restart. At the Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium, a home side that had not lost in the league phase looked comfortable.

Milan's half-time changes then started to matter. Pulisic and Musah came on, and the visitors began to find space on the right. Hutchinson cut the gap on 56 minutes. Seven minutes later his cross was headed in by Cissè, and on 71 minutes another delivery from the same flank was met first time by Pulisic.

Olympiacos threw players forward late on and paid for it. Hutchinson led one more break in stoppage time, Pulisic squared, and Moreira finished low into the corner for 4-2.

## Hutchinson and Pulisic: why Milan came back

![Hutchinson one goal and two assists, Pulisic one goal and one assist vs Olympiacos](/images/olympiacos-milan-uel-md5-section-3-hutchinson-pulisic.jpg)

**The facts.** Hutchinson was directly involved in three of Milan's four goals: one goal and two assists. Pulisic, a half-time substitute, scored the third goal and set up the fourth. All four Milan goals came after the break.

**Our analysis.** Once Olympiacos were ahead, they were happy to defend their box, and that suited Milan. Their best football came on the right, where Hutchinson kept getting one-on-one and delivering early crosses before the defence could reset. The fourth goal was a classic case of a home side chasing an equaliser it no longer needed to chase.

The scoreline flatters Milan's control. They were poor before the break and conceded twice. The second half showed what this side can do when its wide players are running at defenders, not passing around them.

## Milan's Europa League season: from struggle to hope

![AC Milan Europa League season: three defeats without scoring before the 4-2 win at Olympiacos](/images/olympiacos-milan-uel-md5-section-4-milan-season.jpg)

Milan arrived in Greece in trouble. Football Italia reported that they had lost three of their first four league-phase games without scoring, with a 4-0 win at Red Bull Salzburg the only bright spot.

That context makes this result bigger than three points. Coming back from 2-0 down away from home, against a side that had not lost, is the kind of night that can change a campaign. One win does not fix a slow start, though. The first half here showed the same defensive problems.

## What it means for the Europa League table

![Provisional Europa League table: Olympiacos 7th on 10 points, AC Milan 21st on 6](/images/olympiacos-milan-uel-md5-section-5-europa-league-table.jpg)

The win takes Milan to six points from five matches. Sky Sports' table and Goal both placed them 21st after the game, which is inside the play-off places but only just.

Olympiacos stay on 10 points. Both sources had them seventh, still inside the top eight that go straight to the last 16.

The format is simple. The top eight qualify for the last 16 directly, teams ninth to 24th go into knockout play-offs, and the bottom 12 are out. Three rounds remain. These positions were recorded on the night and may move once every matchday 5 game is complete, so check the official UEFA table.
