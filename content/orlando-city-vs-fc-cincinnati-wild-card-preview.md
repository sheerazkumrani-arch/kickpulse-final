## How the MLS Wild Card Round Works

The Wild Card is a single match hosted by the higher seed. Orlando earned that home date by beating New England 1-0 on Decision Day, with Braian Ojeda scoring just before halftime. Cincinnati could not secure the result they needed on the final day. There is no second leg, so every minute counts.

## Three Storylines to Watch

**Griezmann versus Evander.** Orlando's Antoine Griezmann and Cincinnati's Evander are the best-known attackers on show. In a one-off game, whichever star finds space first could swing the night.

**A rivalry with nothing between them.** The two clubs have met 15 times, and the series is level at 5-5-5. A league meeting in Orlando on August 15 ended 1-1. Tonight is their first postseason meeting.

![Orlando City vs FC Cincinnati head-to-head record: 5 wins each and 5 draws in 15 meetings](/images/orlando-city-fc-cincinnati-head-to-head-record.jpg)

**Pressure of different kinds.** This is Orlando's fifth straight playoff appearance, but they lost last year's Wild Card game at Chicago. Cincinnati are in the Wild Card round for the first time, and they are short-handed: Obinna Nwobodo is suspended and Julian Malatini is doubtful.

## What Happens Next
