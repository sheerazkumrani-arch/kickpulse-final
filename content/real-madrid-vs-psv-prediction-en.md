## Why the first goal matters in Real Madrid vs PSV

![Stat graphic: PSV 2 wins in 50 Champions League away games after conceding first](/images/real-madrid-vs-psv-prediction-first-goal-hero.webp)
*PSV's comeback problem away from home (Sports Mole).*

PSV's two wins in 50 Champions League away games after falling behind is a 4% recovery rate. That is the surprising number behind our call.

They have won all eight competitive home games this season and scored four goals in five of them (Sports Mole, data as of 24 Nov 2026). Kylian Mbappé has scored in all four league phase matches.

PSV's best route is the opposite script. Peter Bosz's side must score early, then defend a lead. Their 1-0 win at Porto proves they can manage tight away games.

Madrid are not flawless. Celta Vigo scored after six minutes on Saturday, yet Jude Bellingham and Carlos Espí turned it around. Madrid conceded first and still won. PSV rarely do.

History adds weight. Real Madrid have lost one of eight previous meetings with PSV, and this is the first since the 1988-89 European Cup quarter-final. PSV have won only two of 19 visits to Spain (The Real Champs, data as of 23 Nov 2026).
