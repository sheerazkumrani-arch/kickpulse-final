# Nations League Quarter-Final Draw 2026/27: Who Faces Who?

![Nations League quarter-final draw 2026/27 hero banner: 8 qualified teams, 4 seeded winners, 2 legs in March 2027](/images/nl-qf-2026-27-hero-banner.jpg)

By **Sheraz Khan** · [Pitchside Notes](https://kickpulse-final.vercel.app/) · Last updated: 19 November 2026

The 2026/27 UEFA Nations League quarter-final draw takes place on **Friday 20 November 2026** in Nyon, Switzerland, at 12:00 CET (4:00 PM in Pakistan). France, Germany, England and Portugal are the seeded group winners. They will face Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain or Norway over two legs on 25–30 March 2027. Here is who can meet whom, and the stories behind all eight qualified teams.

![Nations League quarter-final draw 2026/27 date, time and format: group winners and runners-up with points, plus the draw and two-leg timeline](/images/nl-qf-2026-27-draw-format.jpg)

## Nations League Quarter-Final Draw 2026/27: Date, Time and Format

- **Date and time:** Friday 20 November 2026, 12:00 CET (11:00 GMT, 4:00 PM PKT)
- **Venue:** UEFA headquarters, Nyon, Switzerland
- **How to watch:** UEFA.tv and UEFA’s official YouTube channel
- **Seeded (Pot 1):** the four group winners
- **Unseeded (Pot 2):** the four runners-up
- **Key rule:** teams from the same group cannot meet in the quarter-finals
- **First legs:** 25–27 March 2027, hosted by the runners-up
- **Second legs:** 28–30 March 2027, hosted by the group winners

Playing the second leg at home is a real advantage, so finishing first mattered. The same draw also sets the promotion and relegation play-offs.

## Who Has Qualified? Group Winners and Runners-Up

| Group | Seeded winner | Unseeded runner-up |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | France (13 points) | Belgium (12) |
| A2 | Germany (11) | Netherlands (11) |

Germany and the Netherlands finished level on 11 points with Greece, and Germany went top on head-to-head. England and Spain were level on 15 points, and England took first place by beating Spain in Madrid. In Group A4, Norway and Denmark both ended on 9 points, and Norway went second on head-to-head.

From the other side: Spain can draw France, Germany or Portugal. The Netherlands can draw England, France or Portugal. Norway can draw England, France or Germany. Belgium can draw England, Germany or Portugal. Spain cannot meet England, and Portugal cannot meet Norway, because they came from the same groups.

## England and Germany: The Form Teams Heading Into March

England finished top of Group A3 with a 3–2 win in Madrid on 15 November. Thomas Tuchel’s side played the closing stages with ten men, yet held on to end Spain’s 43-match unbeaten run. Harry Kane scored in all six of England’s group games and finished as the league phase’s top scorer with eight goals, while Jude Bellingham led the assists chart with six. England’s 19 goals were the most of any team.

Germany ended the group stage in style. Jürgen Klopp’s team beat the Netherlands 5–0 in Berlin on 16 November, with Florian Wirtz scoring twice, to win Group A2. It was Germany’s biggest win over the Dutch since a 7–0 victory in 1959, and it was the first defeat for the Netherlands since Xavi took charge.

## Norway’s Historic Quarter-Final Place: Haaland’s Late Penalty

Norway reached the Nations League quarter-finals for the first time. The knockout round only began in 2024/25, and Norway were not part of it then. In Copenhagen on 17 November they came from 2–0 down to beat Denmark 3–2. Erling Haaland scored twice, including a penalty in the 96th minute, and Martin Ødegaard equalised in the 84th. Both teams finished on nine points, and Norway took second place on head-to-head.

## Portugal Without Ronaldo: Defending Champions Stay Strong

Portugal won Group A4 with 15 points. They were without Cristiano Ronaldo after he left the squad’s camp in Denmark on 30 September, when coach Jorge Jesus said he would not start. Portugal then won 4–2 in Copenhagen without him. On 5 November, Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football, ending a 23-year career with Portugal.

Portugal then lost 4–1 at home to Denmark on 14 November. They bounced back with a 3–2 win at Wales on 17 November to top the group. Do not expect Ronaldo back for March. Portugal, winners of the 2019 and 2025 Nations League, are now building a new team under Jorge Jesus.

## Nations League Play-Offs: Who Is in Danger?

- **Stay in League A:** Italy (third in Group A1, 10 points) and Greece (third in Group A2, 11 points)
- **League A/B play-offs:** Croatia and Denmark (third-placed), Serbia and Wales (fourth-placed)
- **Opponents in the play-offs:** League B runners-up Slovenia, Hungary, Republic of Ireland and Bosnia and Herzegovina
- **Relegated to League B:** Türkiye and Czechia
- **Promoted to League A:** Switzerland, Northern Ireland, Austria and Sweden

## What Makes the 2026/27 Nations League Special?

The Nations League began in 2018/19 to replace low-stakes friendlies with competitive matches. Portugal won the first edition in 2019 and the 2025 edition. France won in 2021 and Spain in 2023. This season is also a bridge: UEFA has said the competition will move to three leagues in 2028/29, and 2026/27 uses an adjusted promotion and relegation system.

The coaches add to the story: Zinedine Zidane (France), Jürgen Klopp (Germany), Thomas Tuchel (England), Jorge Jesus (Portugal) and Xavi (Netherlands).

## Nations League Draw FAQ

**When is the 2026/27 Nations League quarter-final draw?**
It is on Friday 20 November 2026 in Nyon, Switzerland, at 12:00 CET, which is 4:00 PM in Pakistan.

**Which teams are seeded in the quarter-final draw?**
France, Germany, England and Portugal, the four group winners, are seeded. Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain and Norway are the unseeded runners-up.

**Can England play Spain in the quarter-finals?**
No. England and Spain came from the same group, so they cannot meet in the quarter-finals. England can face Belgium, the Netherlands or Norway.

**When are the Nations League quarter-finals played?**
First legs are on 25–27 March 2027 and second legs on 28–30 March 2027. The group winners host the second leg.

**Which teams are in the League A/B play-offs?**
Croatia, Denmark, Serbia and Wales from League A, against Slovenia, Hungary, Republic of Ireland and Bosnia and Herzegovina from League B.

**Has Cristiano Ronaldo retired from Portugal?**
Yes. He announced his retirement from international football on 5 November 2026 after a 23-year career with Portugal.
