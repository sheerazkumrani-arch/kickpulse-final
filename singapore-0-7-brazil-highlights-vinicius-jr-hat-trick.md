## Singapore 0-7 Brazil: the result at a glance

This friendly closed Brazil's Asian tour, and it came only three days after a chaotic 3-2 defeat to Japan at the same ground. That loss finished with red cards for both Ancelotti and Raphinha, so the Selecao arrived looking for calm as much as goals.

Singapore, ranked 149th in the world, had no answer. Reports described the contest as closer to a training session than a competitive match, and the scoreline reflected it.

## Vinicius Jr hat-trick: three goals in 12 minutes

The first half belonged to the Real Madrid winger. His opener arrived on eight minutes, his second only four minutes later, and the third came on 20 minutes. That gave him three goals in a 12-minute burst, and it was the first senior hat-trick of his Brazil career.

The timing mattered. Vinicius had scored twice against Japan on Saturday, including a superb long-range strike into the top corner, yet finished on the losing side. Here the finishing was just as sharp, but the stakes were far lower and the space far greater. Singapore's back line sat deep, then stepped up too late, and Vinicius punished every gap.

## How the match unfolded

Brazil imposed their game from the first whistle. The home supporters were lively on the rare occasions Singapore reached the Brazilian half, and those moments drew genuine noise from the stands, but the gulf in quality was never in doubt. Brazil kept the ball, moved it quickly into wide areas and found runners between the lines with little resistance.

By half-time the contest was over as a competitive match, and the second half became a showcase of how many different players could hurt a tired, outclassed defence. The tempo dropped, the goals kept coming, and Brazil never looked under pressure.

## The rest of the Brazil goals

Once the hat-trick was complete, the contest was effectively over. Endrick made it four on 30 minutes, adding to a first half that Brazil controlled from start to finish.

After the break, Estevao scored on 50 minutes, Rayan added the sixth on 65, and Allan completed the scoring on 72. Four different scorers beyond the headline man showed how much attacking depth Ancelotti had at his disposal.

## Ancelotti's reaction and what it means

For Ancelotti, the evening was about rhythm and reassurance. The Japan defeat exposed defensive problems, and the sending-off added noise around the squad. A routine win lets him move on without a crisis, although he will know the opposition was far weaker than anything Brazil will face in serious competition.

Raphinha missed this match because of his suspension, a reminder that discipline remains a small worry. Even so, the attacking options looked sharp, with Vinicius, Endrick, Estevao and Rayan all contributing in a single night.
