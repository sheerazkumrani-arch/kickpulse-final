Major League Soccer is the top soccer league in the United States and Canada. If you are new to it, the scoreboard can look a little unfamiliar: two conferences, a long season and a playoff system that works differently from most European leagues. This guide explains how to read Major League Soccer live scores and the league table, step by step.

## What the scoreboard shows

A live scoreboard has a few simple parts. Each match card has the two team names, the score for each team and a status. The status is either a match minute while the game is on, "HT" at half-time or "FT" at full-time. If the game has not started, you will see the kickoff time instead.

On the [MLS live scores](https://kickpulse-football.vercel.app/mls-live-scores/) page, the status updates by itself during matches. Tap any card to open more detail, such as goals, cards, stats and lineups when they are available.

## Why MLS has two conferences

Unlike many leagues, MLS splits its clubs into two groups: the Eastern Conference and the Western Conference. This is partly about geography, because the United States and Canada are huge and travel is long. Teams play most of their matches within their own conference, and the table is shown for each conference.

That is why, when you look at the league table, you will often see two separate lists rather than one. It is not a mistake. It is how the league works.

## How the table works

The table is ordered by points. A win earns three points, a draw earns one and a loss earns none. If two teams have the same points, the tie is broken by other measures, such as wins and goal difference.

Look at these columns when you read the table:

- **P or GP:** games played.
- **W, D, L:** wins, draws and losses.
- **GD:** goal difference, which is goals scored minus goals conceded.
- **Pts:** total points.

Games played matters more than people think. A team lower in the table with two games in hand can overtake a team above it, so always compare the number of games played before drawing conclusions.

## From the table to the playoffs

At the end of the regular season, the top teams in each conference qualify for the MLS Cup playoffs. After that, the competition becomes a knockout, and one result can end a team's season. If you follow a team that is fighting for a playoff place, the table is the most important thing to watch in the last weeks.

## Using the scoreboard well

A few simple habits make the scoreboard more useful:

1. **Use the tabs.** Live shows what is on now, Finished shows what ended, and Upcoming shows what is next.
2. **Use the date bar.** Jump to yesterday for results or tomorrow for the next games.
3. **Use search.** Type a club's name to filter the list, for example Inter Miami.
4. **Open the table.** After each match day, see how the standings changed.
