The UEFA Nations League quarter finals are almost set. Seven of the eight teams are already through, the last place is decided tonight, and the Nations League quarter-finals 2027 matches will be played in March. This guide covers the schedule, the teams, the format and the draw in one place.

![Nations League quarter finals 2026/27 at a glance: draw date, first legs and second legs](/images/nations-league-quarter-finals-2026-27.jpg "Nations League quarter finals 2026/27 at a glance.")

## When are the Nations League quarter finals?

The first legs of the Nations League quarter finals are scheduled for 25 to 27 March 2027, with the second legs on 28 to 30 March. The four winners go through to the Nations League Finals on 9 to 13 June 2027.

Exact kickoff times and stadiums for each tie will be confirmed after the draw.

## Which teams are in the Nations League quarter finals?

The top two teams in each of the four League A groups reach the last eight. After Matchday 6 on Monday, UEFA lists seven teams as qualified: Germany, the Netherlands, France, Belgium, England, Portugal and Spain. France won Group A1, Germany won Group A2 and England won Group A3. Germany and the Netherlands went through on head-to-head records after finishing level on 11 points with Greece.

The eighth place goes to Denmark or Norway. They meet tonight in Group A4, with kickoff at 8:45 pm CET (7:45 pm in the UK and 12:45 am in Pakistan), while Portugal play Wales in the same group. Italy missed out after a 4-1 defeat to Belgium on Sunday.

## Nations League quarter-finals draw 2026

The draw takes place on Friday 20 November 2026 at 11:00 UK time at UEFA's headquarters. That is 4:00 pm in Pakistan and 6:00 am US Eastern. UEFA will also draw the promotion and relegation play-offs, and the draw is shown live on UEFA.tv and UEFA's YouTube channel.

## Nations League quarter finals format and matches

Each tie is played over two legs, home and away. The four group winners are drawn against the four runners-up from different groups, and the group winners host the second leg. A tie level on aggregate can go to penalties, as it did when France beat Croatia in the last edition.

The winners reach the Nations League Finals, a knockout event with semi-finals, a third-place play-off and the final. UEFA's Executive Committee will choose the host. The last Nations League quarter finals matches produced 31 goals, including a 5-5 aggregate draw between the Netherlands and Spain, so expect goals again.

## Players to watch in the quarter finals

Harry Kane scored a first-half penalty as England beat Spain 3-2 in Madrid on Sunday, ending Spain's 43-match unbeaten run. Spain still have Lamine Yamal, who won the penalty that cut the deficit. Florian Wirtz scored in Germany's 5-0 win over the Netherlands, and Jeremy Doku was on the scoresheet as Belgium beat Italy 4-1.

Cristiano Ronaldo will not be part of these Nations League quarter finals. The Portugal captain retired from international football on 5 November 2026 after 234 caps and a record 146 goals. Portugal, who beat Spain in the 2024/25 final, go into the knockout round under head coach Jorge Jesus.
